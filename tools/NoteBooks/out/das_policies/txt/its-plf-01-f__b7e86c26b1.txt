                    ITS-PLF-01-F Standard Ultrabook Notebook


State of Ohio IT Standard - Attachment F

Effective Date:
 10/28/2025

Effective Period:
 12 months

This standard configuration is subject to change within 12 months of its effective date. The most current version of this standard configuration (available on the DAS Technology & Strategy website) should be obtained when making IT planning or acquisition decisions.

Profile for Standard Ultrabook Notebook

Category Description

This category refers to a lightweight notebook with no internal optical drives that is used primarily by mobile workers.

Category Use Cases

Mobile workers that need a lightweight notebook.

Planned Service Life

Notebooks in this category are expected to have a planned service life of four years. Agencies acquiring this category should be able to estimate the total cost of ownership over a four-year period. Systems are expected to be acquired with four-year parts and labor warranty from the manufacturer. Options are available from the manufacturer for upgrading the warranty to onsite, next business day.

Manageability

Notebooks that conform to this category can be centrally managed and locked down to achieve the lowest total cost of ownership. These notebooks are capable of having a factory-installed software image. They can be supported remotely and diagnosed by agencies with the appropriate management tools. Intel® vPro features with Intel® Active Management Technology are recommended.
