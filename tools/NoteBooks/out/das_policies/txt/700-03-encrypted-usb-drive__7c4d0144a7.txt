I. Purpose 
The purpose of this standard is to ensure that only Ohio Department of Administrative Services 
(DAS) Office of Information Technology (OIT) approved encrypted universal serial bus (USB) drives 
are used to store Confidential  D ata or Personally Identifiable Information. The requirements 
outlined in this standard will help DAS OIT technical teams ensure that encrypted USB drives are 
consistently implemented across the agency . The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy 
body outside of a title or heading is in bold, italic type , and is hyperlinked to the definition in 
Section IV. 
II. Sc
ope  
This standard applies to the storage of Confidential Data on USB drives and represents the 
m
inimum acceptable criteria governing DAS encrypted USB drives. 
III. Standard 
A
. Encryption Standard for DAS -m anaged encrypted USB drives : DAS OIT approved 
encrypted USB drives shall meet the following requirements: 
 
1. The encryption technology shall conform to Ohio IT Standard ITS- SEC- 01, "Data 
Encryption and Cryptography." 
2. The implementation and management of the drives shall be in accordance with Ohio 
Administrative Policy IT-14, "Data Encryption and Securing Confidential Data."

3. Each drive shall be configured with an independent Ad ministration Personal 
Identification Number (PIN)  for access. The administration PIN will be set and 
maintained by DAS IT Service (ITS). 
4. Users shall set their own User Access PIN that will unlock the encrypted USB drive. 
5. Encrypted USB drives shall prevent Users from setting PINs that contain consecutive 
numbers. 
6. Encrypted USB drives shall be purchased and managed through DAS ITS. 
 
B. Implementation 
1. All new DAS encrypted USB drives shall be purchased and managed through DAS ITS. 
2. Any existing USB drives that are used to store Confidential Data shall be turned into DAS 
IT Services and be replaced with a drive that meets the requirements outlined in this 
standard. DAS IT Services shall ensure that the data on the drive is securely handled in 
accordance with state and agency policies.  
 
IV. Definitions 
 
A. Administration Personal Identification Number (PIN) . This is a PIN associated with the 
encrypted USB drive that allows DAS OIT IT Services to manage User access. 
B. Confidential Data. This classification includes Data that is required to be protected by law or 
regulation, is intended for confidential use, and may not be copied or removed from an 
agency's operational control without authorized permission.  This classification includes Data 
that, if compromised, may result in loss of life, serious injury, or other harm to an individual 
or group, or disruption to critical agency operations. 
C. Personally Identifiable Information. Information that can be used directly or in combination 
with other information to identify a particular individual. It includes: 
 
• a name, identifying number, symbol, or other identifier assigned to a person,  
• any information that describes anything about a person,  
• any information that indicates actions done by or to a person, and  
• any information that indicates that a person possesses certain personal characteristics.1 
D. User. For the purposes of this policy, a User  is defined as employees, contractors, temporary 
personnel and other agents of the state who administer and use state computer and 
telecommunications systems on behalf of the state.
