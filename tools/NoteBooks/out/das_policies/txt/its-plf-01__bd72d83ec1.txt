                    ITS-PLF-01 Enterprise Client Computer Hardware


State of Ohio IT Standard

Enterprise Client Computer Hardware

No: ITS-PLF-01
 - Information Technology

Effective:
 10/28/2025

Approved By:
 Katrina Flory, State CIO/DAS Assistant Director

Purpose

This state IT standard defines the hardware requirements and standard configurations for client computers and monitors purchased by state agencies for use by state workers. This standard does not apply to computers purchased for use as servers.

Scope

This standard applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).

Standard

Agencies must acquire client computers and monitors that conform to standard configurations, including the mandatory security and environmental features defined by this state IT standard and its associated attachments. Client computer hardware must meet and not exceed the standard configurations defined in the attachments to this standard.

Procurement specifications and requests for quotations that contain hardware attributes, features or peripheral devices not specified in the standard configurations must not result in a configuration that conflicts with or usurps this standard.

The following sections describe the client computer categories for which there are published standard configurations and specifications.

Desktop Categories

The Standard Business Class Desktop category is the most common configuration and is intended to meet a wide range of state worker requirements. It offers the capacity and resources needed to do the job for the majority of the state government workforce.

The Specialty Use Class Desktop category is intended to fill the requirements for specific job purposes. This category offers significant capacity and the expandability required to meet demanding and very high-end computing requirements. The acquisition of this configuration is limited to certain use cases and requires that a business justification be attached to each purchase request.

Notebook Categories

The Standard Mobile Notebook category is intended for the mobile workforce. The processor and hard drive are optimized for extended battery life. These workers may use their notebooks in a variety of non-office environments, including hotels, airports and customer sites.

The Specialty Use Notebook category is oriented towards system performance and may result in reduced battery life. The processor and hard drive are optimized for higher performance. This category offers the capacity required to meet demanding and very high-end computing requirements. The acquisition of this configuration is limited to certain use cases and requires that a business justification be attached to each purchase request.

The Standard Convertible Tablet Notebook category is intended for mobile workers using pen-based applications. It is a notebook computer capable of taking input using a stylus or touch screen, or both. The acquisition of this configuration is limited to certain use cases and requires that a business justification be attached to each purchase request.

The Standard Ultrabook Notebook category refers to a lightweight notebook with no internal optical drives and is used primarily by mobile workers.

Monitor Specifications

Monitor displays used with client computers must meet and not exceed the specifications defined in the attachment to this standard.

Revisions to this Standard

The client computer hardware standard must be regularly reviewed and updated to reflect changes in technology, market trends, agency requirements, and state policies as well as based on recommendations from the Endpoint Computing Hardware Working Group.

Exceptions to this Standard

Agencies requesting to procure client computers and monitors not in conformance with this state IT standard must provide sufficient business justification to document why a standard configuration does not satisfy their business requirements. In general, no exceptions will be granted to the mandatory security features and environmental compliance and labeling. An automatic exception is allowed for client computer configurations that conform to a prior version of a standard configuration.

Implementation

Agencies using OhioBuys should shop from the contract catalogs for standard-based client computers and monitors. Shopping from the catalogs will allow DAS to track the spend associated with these items. (Refer to the standard attachments for links to the available contract catalogs.)

			Agencies should use contracts for standard-based client computers and monitors negotiated by DAS IT Procurement Services unless they can determine more favorable pricing for the same or superior products from alternative sources.

Manufacturers, contractors, and resellers supplying client computers and monitors should understand that Agencies superintended by DAS OIT are required to purchase products that conform to this state IT standard. Suppliers should become familiar with the standard and assist Agencies with standard-based configurations and pricing.
