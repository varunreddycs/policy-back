OFMGUD0026  REV 02/21  
 
 
Fleet Coordinator Role 
 
The Fleet Coordinator will act as the primary contact between agency management/operations and the 
Office of Fleet Management. 
Vehicle Assignments 
 Work with agency personnel to ensure vehicle assignment information is properly entered and 
maintained.   
 Work with agency personnel to ensure that vehicle location information is properly entered and 
maintained. 
Motor Pool dispatching (if applicable) 
 Responsible for coordinating operator id requests. 
 Dispatching and returning vehicles (if applicable) 
 If not directly responsible for Motor pool operations, ensure staff is properly trained to perform 
Dispatching and Returning of motor pool vehicles. 
 Ensure that agency pool vehicles are properly maintained. 
Fleet Card 
 Coordinate processing of card reconciliation packets from the operators.  Packets are due 10 
days from the end of the month. 
 Assist in the distribution of new and replacement fleet cards to agency employees. 
Reporting 
 Access to reporting module in FleetOhio to run standard and ad hoc reports. 
 Ability to research vehicle data in FleetOhio. 
Internal Fuel Tanks (if applicable) 
 Responsible for monitoring and the processing of internal fuel tank transactions. 
 Responsible for ensuring the processing of fuel delivery receipts. 
New Vehicle Processing 
 Coordinate the pickup and return of vehicles as they are delivered. 
 Processing of agency lease agreements. 
Training Requirements 
 State Fleet Information Management System (FleetOhio) 
o Basic Vehicle Lookup, Assignment, and Reporting 
o View Fuel and Work Orders 
 Fleet Commander (Voyager) 
o Transaction detail reports
