                  Employee Home Address Policy (HR-17)


August 29, 2023

 |

Agency

State of Ohio Administrative Policy

No: HR-17 -
 State Human Resources Division

Effective:
 August 29, 2023

Issued By:
Kathleen C. Madden, Director

Purpose

To require all state employees to have a valid home address on file with the State of Ohio. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV.

Scope

This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).

Policy

It is the policy of the State of Ohio to require all employees to have a valid home address on file with the State of Ohio. All Agencies are responsible for ensuring that employees have a valid home address on file that is not a P.O. Box. While an employee may continue to list a P.O. Box as a mailing address, an employee may not use a P.O. Box as a home address except employees in the
Safe at Home Program
 as outlined in Section A below.

Safe at Home Program

Only an employee who is a
Program Participant
 in the Safe at Home Program through the Ohio Secretary of State and has a
Confidential Address
 designated by the Ohio Secretary of State pursuant to Ohio Revised Code (ORC) sections 111.41 through 111.99 may use a P.O. Box as a home address. This is the only exception.

A Program Participant may request through their Agency human resources office to use the address designated by the Ohio Secretary of State as their home address. It is the responsibility of the Program Participant to contact their Agency human resources office to make such a request, and to provide their human resources contact with verification of the P.O. Box address and unique lot number provided by the Ohio Secretary of State. If an Agency has a Safe at Home Program Participant’s actual home address on file for any reason, the human resources office should work with Agency legal counsel to ensure it is kept confidential.

Pursuant to ORC 111.42, the approval for such a P.O. Box is only valid for four years after the date of certification by the Ohio Secretary of State unless the certification is withdrawn or invalidated before the end of the four-year period. Agencies should work with Program Participants to update address information, if the certification is withdrawn, invalidated, or expires and is not renewed.

Changing Home Address

An employee can change their home address by contacting their Agency human resources office or through myOhio.gov. A link to the myOhio.gov log-in page can be found in Section VI below.

An email confirming the change of address will be sent to the email address on file for the employee as well as the Agency benefits contact.

If the employee has questions about changing their address online, the employee should contact the Agency human resources office or the Ohio Department of Administrative Services, Human Resources Customer Service, at 1-800-409-1205, option 2.

If an employee is adding a P.O. Box provided by the Ohio Secretary of State, the employee must provide verification of program participation approval to the Agency’s human resources office.

Definitions

Confidential Address:
 Pursuant to ORC 111.41, the address of a “Safe at Home” Program Participant’s residence, school, institution of higher education, business, or place of employment, as specified on an application to be a Safe at Home Program Participant through the Ohio Secretary of State or on a notice of change of address filed with the Secretary of State under ORC 111.42. A Confidential Address is not a public record under ORC 149.43 and shall be kept confidential.

Program Participant:
 A person who is certified by the Ohio Secretary of State as a Safe at Home Program Participant under ORC 111.42.

Safe at Home Program:
 The program established in sections ORC 111.41 through 111.99, Substitute House Bill 359, 131st General Assembly that was signed into law on June 8, 2016. The program provides confidentiality protections for victims of domestic violence, menacing by stalking, human trafficking, trafficking in persons, rape, or sexual battery and allows wireless service account transfer in a domestic violence situation. The law allows victims to apply to the Ohio Secretary of State to have their address kept confidential. The Ohio Secretary of State will provide approved Program Participants a P.O. Box and unique lot number which can be used as their official address. Upon request by a Program Participant, any governmental entity must use the address designated by the Ohio Secretary of State. The P.O. Box will be housed with the Ohio Secretary of State and the Ohio Secretary of State will be responsible for ensuring that all first-class mail is forwarded to Program Participants from their P.O. Box to their actual residential address. More information about the Safe at Home program can be found via the link in the Resources section below.
