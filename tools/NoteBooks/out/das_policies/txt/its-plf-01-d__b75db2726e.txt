                    ITS-PLF-01-D Specialty Use Notebook


State of Ohio IT Standard - Attachment D

Effective Date:
 10/28/2025

Effective Period:
 12 months

This standard configuration is subject to change within 12 months of its effective date. The most current version of this standard configuration (available on the DAS Technology & Strategy website) should be obtained when making IT planning or acquisition decisions.

Profile for Specialty Use Notebook

Category Description

This category is oriented towards system performance, which may result in reduced battery life. The processor and hard drive are optimized for higher performance. This category offers the significant capacity required to meet demanding computing requirements.
The acquisition of this configuration is limited to certain use cases and requires that a business justification be attached to each purchase request.

Category Use Cases

Staff who primarily work in one location and typically connect their system to a power outlet, but still need the mobility of a notebook for occasional travel.

Developers who design and maintain mission critical applications.

System personnel who operate and support mission critical services or large-scale infrastructures.

Users who work with software applications including, but not limited to, geographic information systems (GIS), spatial analysis, scientific analysis, computer-aided design, complex graphic design, video editing and creation, large-scale data modeling, statistical analysis and data mining.

Planned Service Life

Notebooks in this category are expected to have a planned service life of four years. Agencies acquiring this category should estimate the total cost of ownership over a four-year period. Systems are expected to be acquired with a four-year parts and labor warranty from the manufacturer. Options are available for upgrading the warranty to onsite, next business day.

Manageability

Notebooks that conform to this category can be centrally managed and locked down to achieve the lowest total cost of ownership. These notebooks are capable of having a factory-installed software image. They can be supported remotely and diagnosed by agencies with the appropriate management tools. Intel® vPro features with Intel® Active Management Technology are recommended.
