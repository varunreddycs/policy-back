                    DAS OISP Approved Security Notification for IT Systems


The document is the DAS OISP-Approved Security Notification for IT Systems. In accordance with Department of Administrative Services Policy 2100-11, "Access Controls," information systems that require authentication shall display an approved system use notification message or banner before granting a user access to the system. The notification message or banner shall be modeled after the DAS OISP-Approved Security Notification for IT Systems.
