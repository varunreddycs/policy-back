I. Purpose  
This policy establishes requirements for asset management activities by State Agencies. The first 
occurrence of a defined term in this policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition 
in Section IV. 
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to State Agencies  required by Ohio Revised Code (ORC) 125.16 to maintain 
current and accurate records of all tangible  Personal Property and Real Property that they hold 
either directly or on the State’s behalf. 
 
III. Policy 
 
A. Asset Management Handbook : State Agencies  shall follow the policies and procedures 
established by the Ohio Department of Administrative Services (DAS) that are contained in the 
State of Ohio Asset Management Handbook  (https://das.ohio.gov/static/property-
services/asset-management-services/am-services/AMS%20Handbook%20%20Updated%20 
(2022_1_28).pdf).  
 
B. State Agency Personnel Roles and Responsibilities 
 
1. State Agency Directors or their designees shall assign: 
a) One or more Inventory Control Officers  for stewardship of State-owned personal 
property. 
b) One or more Inventory Control Officers for stewardship of State-owned Real Property 
and Infrastructure.

2. State Agency Directors  or their designees shall give Inventory Control Officers the 
appropriate level of authority necessary for accurately and properly maintaining the ir 
agencies’ inventory management systems and processes.  
3. Inventory Control Officers shall be the primary points of contact between their agencies and 
DAS for inventory-related issues and information.  
4. State Agency D irectors or their designees may also assign additional staff members as 
Inventory Coordinators , Personal Property (Equipment) Custodians , and Physical 
Inventory Specialists to assist with their agencies’ asset management activities. 
 
C. Certifications 
 
1. State Agencies shall annually certify their State property inventory activities. Certifications 
shall cover the period of July 1 – June 30 and be: 
a) Completed as prescribed by the State of Ohio Asset Management Handbook. 
b) Filed with DAS on or before October 1. 
2. State Agencies shall biennially conduct and certify a physical inventory and reconcile their 
perpetual inventory records in OAKS AM or their in-house asset management systems.  
a) A physical inventory and reconciliation can be completed at any time during the 
biennium.  
b) After the close of a biennium, certifications must be completed and filed with DAS on or 
before October 1.  
 
D. Letters of Representation: Before leaving office, ORC 117.17 requires State Agency Directors to 
prepare a Letter of Representation for their successors. Information about the content and 
submission of Letters of Representation  is on the Ohio Auditor of State  (AOS) webpage 
(https://ohioauditor.gov/resources/AOSNotifications.html).  
 
IV. Definitions 
 
A. Infrastructure. Long -lived capital assets that are stationary in nature and normally can be 
preserved for a significantly greater number of years than most capital assets (e.g., roads, 
bridges, tunnels, drainage systems, etc.).  
B. Inventory Control Officers. State Agency staff members designated by their agency Directors or 
their designees to follow inventory management policies and procedures and maintain 
inventory records on behalf of their agencies in accordance with ORC 125.16.  
C. Inventory Coordinator. State Agency staff members who assist Inventory Control Officers with 
maintaining a portion of their agencies’ inventories. An Inventory Coordinator’s duties and 
tasks are assigned by the agency’s Inventory Control Officer. 
D. Letter of Representation. As required by ORC 117.17, an inventory-related letter prepared by an 
agency Director before leaving office , which is completed and distributed in a manner 
prescribed by the AOS.  
E. Personal Property . Includes every tangible thing that is the subject of ownership, whether 
animate or inanimate, including a business fixture, and that does not constitute Real Property

F. Personal Property (Equipment) Custodians. A Custodian is a State Agency staff member who is 
a primary user and/or caretaker of equipment. Primary users are staff members who personally 
use the equipment on a daily basis. Caretakers are  staff members who oversee shared 
equipment used by a designated group or organizational unit. Personal Property (Equipment) 
Custodians are responsible for notifying either Inventory Coordinators or Inventory Control 
Officers when an assets are relocated.  
G. Physical Inventory Specialist s. Agency staff members assigned to take physical inventory of 
State property.  
H. Real Property . Land and whatever is erected or affixed to land (above or below), excluding 
infrastructure. Major classifications of Real Property  are land, land improvements , and 
buildings.  
I. State Agencies. Every organized body, office, or agency established by the laws of the State for 
the exercise of any function of state government. It does not include elected state officers, the 
general assembly or any legislative agency, a court or any judicial agency, or a state institution 
of higher education.
