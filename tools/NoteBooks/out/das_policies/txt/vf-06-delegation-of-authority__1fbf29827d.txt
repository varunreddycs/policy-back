I. Purpose 
The Department of Administrative Services Office of Fleet Management (OFM) may delegate any 
or all of its duties regarding Fleet Management  to a State Agency  if the State Agency 
demonstrates it meets the requirements set forth in this policy. This policy specifies the 
requirements for full delegation, partial delegation, and withdrawal of delegation of Fleet 
Management duties to a State Agency. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in 
bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV. 
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to all State Agencies that utilize state fleet vehicles, unless deemed exempt by 
statute. 
 
III. Policy 
OFM manages the fleet of all State Agencies that utilize state fleet vehicles unless OFM delegates 
that management to the State Agency.  
 
A. Full Delegation of Authority to a State Agency: A State Agency may request full delegation 
of the management of its fleet and OFM may grant full delegation if the State Agency meets 
the requirements set forth below. OFM’s delegation will be provided in writing to the State 
Agency and will be effective for no more than three years. 
 
1. Employs a certified fleet manager as specified in DAS Statewide Policy  VF-07/Fleet 
Manager Certification Program 
2. Demonstrates the ability to use all fleet management information systems, update all 
fleet management information systems in a timely manner, and generate 
comprehensive reports from fleet management information systems

3. Demonstrates the ability to use the fleet credit card system as specified in DAS

4. Demonstrates the ability to enter bulk -fueling data into the fleet management

5. Demonstrates the ability to regularly analyze motor vehicle cost per mile and miles per

Agency's fleet 
6. Demonstrates the ability to regularly analyze motor vehicle mileage reimbursements 
requested by OFM 
7. Demonstrates the ability to develop and submit an approved comprehensive fleet plan 
and to provide the comprehensive plan on a timely basis 
8. Demonstrates adequate processes and procedures to ensure Fleet Management 
reporting and fiscal integrity for the administration of an on -site maintenance facility, 
if applicable 
9. Demonstrates that the State A gency has an individual that can assume, and keep 
current, the duties of the agency's certified fleet manager in their absence 
 
B. Partial Delegation of Authority to a State Agency: If the State Agency employs a certified 
fleet manager or a provisionally certified fleet manager as specified in VF -07 but does not 
demonstrate compliance with all other requirements set forth in section III.A. of this Policy, 
the State Agency may request partial delegation of authority to manage its fleet and OFM 
may grant partial delegation, which will be effective for no more than three years. If OFM 
partially delegates authority,  it will provide the partial delegation in writing to the State 
Agency with the following information: 
 
1. The requirements in Section III.A. that the State Agency does not meet 
2. The deadline for the State Agency to provide to OFM a work plan detailing specific goals, 
tasks, and timelines that will allow the State A gency to receive full delegation of 
authority, which OFM will evaluate annually 
3. An updated reasonable fee, if any, for the Fleet Management functions provided to the 
State Agency by OFM 
 
C. No Delegation of Authority to a State Agency: If OFM determines to fully manage the State 
Agency's fleet, OFM will advise the State Agency in writing of the determination and will 
inform the State Agency of the following: 
 
1. The requirements in Section III.A. that the State Agency does not meet 
2. The deadline to provide to OFM a work plan detailing specific goals, tasks, and timelines 
that will allow the State Agency to receive full delegation of authority 
3. A reasonable fee, if any, for the Fleet M anagement functions provided to the State 
Agency by OFM

D. Removal of Delegation of Authority to a State Agency 
 
1. OFM may withdraw its delegation 
a. if it determines that the State Agency no longer demonstrates or complies with the 
requirements set forth in Section III.A.  
b. upon expiration of the delegation period if the State Agency fails to comply with its 
work plan 
c. if the State Agency fails to timely submit all reports required by OFM 
d. for any other reason OFM deems appropriate 
2. If OFM decides to withdraw the delegation, it will provide the following information to 
the State Agency in writing: 
a. notice that OFM will manage the State Agency’s fleet; 
b. the reason(s) for the withdrawal of the delegation; 
c. a written description of the responsibilities of each party; 
d. the deadline to provide to OFM a corrective work plan that details the specific goals, 
tasks, and timelines that will allow the State Agency to regain delegation of 
authority; and 
e. a reasonable fee, if any, for the Fleet Management functions provided to the State 
Agency by OFM. 
 
IV. Definitions 
 
A. Fleet Management . T he process of organizing, coordinating, and overseeing vehicles  
owned by the state. It includes maintenance, acquiring vehicles, managing drivers, tracking 
vehicle movements, and ensuring compliance with regulations. 
B. State Agency . E very organized body, office, board, authority, commission, or agency 
established by the laws of the state for the exercise of any governmental or quasi -
governmental function of state government regardless of the funding source for that entity, 
other than any state institution of higher education, the office of the governor, lieutenant 
governor, auditor of state, treasurer of state, secretary of state, attorney general, general 
assembly or any legislative agency, the courts or any judicial agency, or any state retirement 
system or retirement program established by or referenced in the Ohio Revised Code.
