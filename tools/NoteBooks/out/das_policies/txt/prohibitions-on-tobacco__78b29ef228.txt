                  Prohibitions on Tobacco and Electronic Nicotine Delivery Systems in the Workplace (HR-48)


August 31, 2023

 |

Agency

State of Ohio Administrative Policy

No: HR-48 -
State Human Resources Division

Effective:
August 31, 2023

Issued By:

Kathleen C. Madden, Director

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to provide guidance to all State of Ohio employees, contractors, and visitors about the prohibition on the use of
Tobacco Products
 and
Electronic Nicotine Delivery Systems (ENDS)
 in or on all state-owned, operated, or leased property, including vehicles. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV below.

Scope

The prohibitions contained in this policy apply to all State of Ohio employees, contractors, and visitors.

Policy

It is the policy of the State of Ohio to promote good health and to protect the general wellbeing of all State of Ohio employees, contractors, and the visiting public by providing a tobacco and nicotine-free environment.

Prohibition on the Use of Tobacco and ENDS Products

Smoking
, or the use of any other tobacco and/or ENDS products in or on all state-owned, operated, or leased property, including vehicles, is prohibited, except for designated outdoor smoking areas. The sale and/or purchase of any tobacco or ENDS product while on state-owned, operated, or leased property, including vehicles, is also prohibited.

Smoking Cessation

Use of
Smoking Cessation Products
 for purposes of cessation and not otherwise inconsistent with this policy is permitted in state owned, operated, or leased property, including vehicles. Only use of smoking cessation products that are approved as smoking cessation products by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) are permitted.

ENDS products, such as
Electronic Cigarettes (E-Cigarettes)
 or
Vaporizers (including Vape Pens)
, are not FDA-approved smoking cessation products. Accordingly, use of any ENDS product on or in state owned, operated, or leased property, including vehicles, is prohibited, except for designated outdoor smoking areas.

Agency Policies

Agencies may create an agency-specific policy regarding the use of Tobacco Products. However, the agency policy may not be less restrictive than this statewide policy.

Definitions

Electronic Cigarettes (E-Cigarettes):
 A battery-operated device that is designed to vaporize nicotine, flavoring, and/or other chemicals that are inhaled by the user.

Electronic Nicotine Delivery Systems (ENDS):
 A noncombustible tobacco product that may contain nicotine, which uses a heated liquid to create an aerosol that is inhaled by the user. This may include, but is not limited to, vapes, Vaporizers, vape pens, hookah pens, Electronic Cigarettes, and e-pipes.

Smoking:
 As defined in Chapter 3794 of the Revised Code, means inhaling, exhaling, burning, or carrying any lighted cigar, cigarette, pipe, or other lighted Smoking device for burning tobacco or any other plant.

Smoking Cessation Products:
 Products that are currently approved by the FDA to help individuals quit Smoking and/or to quit using other Tobacco Products. Currently, use of Electronic Cigarettes is not approved by the FDA for cessation or for any other purpose.

Tobacco Products:
 Any item containing or derived from tobacco, including but not limited to:

Cigarettes (including, cloves, bidis, kreteks)

Cigars/Cigarillos

Tobacco Pipes

Oral Tobacco

Nasal Tobacco

Any other form of smokeless tobacco

Any product intended to mimic Tobacco Products, containing tobacco flavoring, or otherwise delivering nicotine other than for cessation purposes.

Vaporizers (including Vape Pens):
 A battery-operated device consisting of a cartridge with a heating element which produces a vapor from a liquid which may contain nicotine, allowing the user to inhale the aerosol vapor.

Authority

ORC 3794.02
OAC 123:1-45-01, 3701-52
