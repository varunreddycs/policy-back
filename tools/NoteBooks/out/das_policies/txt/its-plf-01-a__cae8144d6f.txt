                    ITS-PLF-01-A Standard Business Class Desktop


State Of Ohio IT Standard - Attachment A

Effective Date:
 October 28, 2025

Effective Period:
 12 months

This standard configuration is subject to change within 12 months of its effective date. The most current version of this standard configuration (available on the DAS Technology & Strategy website) should be obtained when making IT planning or acquisition decisions.

Profile for Standard Business Class Desktop

Category Description

This category is the most common desktop configuration and is intended to meet a wide range of state worker requirements. It offers the capacity and resources needed to do the job for the majority of the state government workforce.

Category Use Cases

State workers who use word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, e-mail, Web applications and Web browsing.

Remote staff, mobile workers, and other non-desk-based workers who share a computer for administrative and other purposes.

Classroom and training room computers without a state worker assigned as a full-time user.

Knowledge workers, analysts, or researchers who work with large documents, complex presentations, database applications and third-party applications.

Planned Service Life

Desktops in this category are expected to have a planned service life of five years. Agencies acquiring this category should estimate the total cost of ownership over a five-year period. Systems are expected to be acquired with a four-year onsite, next-business-day warranty from the manufacturer.

Manageability

Desktops that conform to this category can be centrally managed and locked down to achieve the lowest total cost of ownership. These desktops are capable of having a factory-installed software image; however, there may be one or more custom applications that are installed after the system is received. They can be supported remotely and diagnosed by agencies with appropriate management tools. Intel® vPro features with Intel® Active Management Technology are recommended.
