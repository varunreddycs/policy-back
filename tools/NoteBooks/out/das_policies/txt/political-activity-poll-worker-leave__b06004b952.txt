                  Poll Worker Leave (HR-23)


August 29, 2023

 |

Agency

State of Ohio Administrative Policy

No: HR-23 -
State Human Resources Division

Effective:
August 29, 2023

Issued By:
Kathleen C. Madden, Director

Purpose

To encourage agencies to implement a leave program for employees who wish to serve as a precinct election official on Election Day, pursuant to Ohio Revised Code (ORC) section 3501.28. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV.

Scope

This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies). Nothing in this policy supersedes or negates any provision of a collective bargaining agreement in effect pursuant to ORC 4117.

Policy

It is the policy of the State of Ohio to allow the appointing authority of an Agency the discretion to determine whether poll worker leave will apply to its Agency employees. If the appointing authority determines that its employees will be eligible for poll worker leave, this policy provides general parameters under which poll worker leave may function for Agency employees. Employee use of poll worker leave is subject to terms and conditions established by the appointing authority for which the employee works.

Responsibility of the Appointing Authority of a State Agency, Board, or Commission

If the appointing authority of a state Agency chooses to permit an employee to use poll worker leave, the appointing authority must establish terms and conditions (a policy) for the leave program (see model policy in Attachment 1). The policy must include a standard procedure to decide which employees are permitted to receive poll worker leave if multiple employees request to use the leave. This procedure must be consistently and uniformly applied to all similarly situated employees.

Additionally, the policy should also address the procedure by which an approval may be revoked due to operational needs, the order of consideration for this type of leave compared to the other types of leaves available to employees, and any other elements needed to address Agency-specific issues.

Employee Eligibility

Permanent full-time and permanent part-time employees who are officially appointed by the board of elections to serve as a precinct election official in a
General Election
,
Primary Election
 or
Special Election
 are eligible to utilize poll worker leave.

No other appointment types are eligible for poll worker leave under this policy.

An employee who is already utilizing any type of paid or unpaid leave is not eligible for poll worker leave.

Approved poll worker leave is to be coded “POLLV” in the Ohio Administrative Knowledge System (OAKS) or KRONOS by Agency payroll staff in the Agency where the poll worker leave was approved.

Leave and Benefit Amount

Employees may receive paid leave for time serving as a precinct election official on an hour-for-hour basis, up to the maximum number of hours the employee was regularly scheduled to work on the day of the election. For example, an employee must work eight hours as a precinct election official in order to receive eight hours of poll worker leave. If an employee serves as a precinct election official for five hours, then the employee will only receive five hours of poll worker leave. Also, if an employee is regularly scheduled to work eight hours and works longer than eight hours at the polls on elections day, the employee is only entitled to eight hours of poll worker leave.

Any discrepancy in the amount of time an employee is regularly scheduled to work and the amount of time the employee serves as a precinct election official will be considered time during which the employee was absent without leave (AWOL). An employee in AWOL status may be subject to discipline, up to and including removal. Discrepancies will be determined from the verification documentation submitted by the employee (see section III.E. below).

Compensation for poll worker leave will be at the employee’s
Regular Rate of Pay
. Unless otherwise specified, employees using poll worker leave will remain in active pay status like any other form of paid leave and continue accruing vacation, personal, and sick time at the normal rate.

Request for Leave

This policy does not alter Agency-specific policies and procedures for requesting time off. Employees requesting poll worker leave must use the standard electronic or written request for leave form (see Resources section below for the location of the request for leave form) and select “Poll Worker Leave” on the electronic form or mark the box for “Other” on the written form, and document on the form that the request is for “Poll Worker Leave.”

In addition to the request for leave, the employee must submit a copy of the certificate of appointment issued by the board of elections, commitment card, or other similar form of confirmation from the board of elections that the employee will be serving as a precinct election official on the Election Day for which the employee is requesting leave. No request for leave will be granted unless the employee submits a confirmation form.

Verification Documentation

In each instance that an employee utilizes poll worker leave, the employee must verify that the amount of leave requested was spent serving as a precinct election official. On the workday following the election on which the employee returns to work, the employee shall submit to the appropriate Agency-designated personnel a properly completed and signed Poll Worker Leave Verification Form (see Resources section below for the location of a sample form).

Additionally, within five working days of receiving payment from the local board of elections, the employee shall submit proof of such payment to the appropriate Agency-designated personnel. Verification will not be considered timely if submitted more than six months beyond the date the employee utilized poll worker leave. Failure of the employee to timely submit the verification stipulated above will cause the employee to be considered absent without leave (AWOL) for that day and may subject the employee to discipline, up to and including removal. Should there be a question regarding whether an employee actually worked as a precinct election official for a particular election, the Agency should verify the date and number of hours worked by the employee with the appropriate county board of elections.

Employees Whose State Agency Does Not Adopt Poll Worker Leave

An employee of an Agency that does not have poll worker leave may request to use personal leave, vacation leave or compensatory time to serve as a precinct election official. Normal Agency policy and procedure for granting time off will apply in these circumstances.

Definitions

General Election:
 The election held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in each November.

Primary Election:
 An election held for the purpose of nominating persons as candidates of political parties for election to offices, and for the purpose of electing persons as member of the controlling committees of political parties and as delegates and alternates to the conventions of political parties. Primary Elections shall be held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in May of each year except in years in which a presidential Primary Election is held.

Regular Rate of Pay:
 The employee’s base rate of pay plus any applicable pay supplements.

Special Election:
 Any election other than those elections defined in other divisions of this section. A Special Election may be held only on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in February, May, August, or November, or on the day authorized by a particular municipal or county charter for the holding of a Primary Election, except that in any year in which a presidential Primary Election is held, no Special Election shall be held in February or May, except as authorized by a municipal or county charter, but may be held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in March.

Attachment 1 - Poll Worker Leave Model Work Policy

Note:
This is a model rule, and it is intended that this will be modified and adopted by each Agency to meet its specific needs within the context of the State of Ohio Administrative Policy on Poll Worker Leave and the applicable statute (ORC 3501.22).

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish procedures in accordance with the
State of Ohio Administrative Policy on Poll Worker Leave (HR-23)
.

Policy Statement

(Agency) recognizes that there is a need for a pool of available individuals to serve as precinct election officials in order for our democratic process to operate in a fair and efficient manner. Accordingly, (Agency) will allow its permanent full-time and part-time employees, certified by the local board of elections, to be eligible for paid leave (poll worker leave) on Election Day to serve as precinct election officials. The ability to be away from work as contemplated by this work rule is subject to the policies and procedures set forth below.

Applicability

Poll worker leave only applies to individuals who work full-time or part-time for (Agency). Employees who are classified as non-permanent appointment types such as seasonal, intermittent, etc., are not eligible for poll worker leave. This work rule shall be uniformly applied to all similarly situated employees. An employee who is on any type of paid or unpaid leave of absence is not eligible for poll worker leave.

Procedures

Employees requesting poll worker leave and supervisors who receive the requests for leave must follow the procedures established by this work rule.

A request for paid time off to serve as a precinct election official on Election Day shall be submitted to the employee’s immediate supervisor at least 14 calendar days prior to the date of the election or as soon as practicable. To request time off the employee must complete the standard electronic or written request for leave form and select “Poll Worker Leave” on the electronic form or check the box for “Other” on the written form and write “Poll Worker Leave” on the form.
Additionally, the employee must submit his or her supervisor a copy of the employee’s certificate of appointment issued by the board of elections pursuant to RC 3501.27(A), a commitment card, or another form of confirmation from the local board of elections that the employee will be serving as a precinct election official on a particular Election Day. Any request for time off that is not timely or presented in the proper manner shall be denied.

Upon receiving a properly completed request for leave form with the required documentation, the immediate supervisor shall note the date and time the request was received in the supervisor’s “Remarks” section. The supervisor is responsible for notifying an employee when the form is not completed properly or when documentation is missing. Such notification shall be given by the supervisor prior to marking the leave form as being received for processing.

Requests for poll worker leave shall be subject to the operational needs of the employee’s work unit. Requests for poll worker leave will be given the lowest priority as compared to all other types of leave requests received from employees within the work unit for the same date.

If the number of employees requesting poll worker leave would cause a work unit to not be able to fulfill its operational needs, then requests for poll worker leave will be granted in the order they were received. The supervisor’s date and time in the remarks section is the sole factor in determining when a request was submitted. Incomplete forms or forms with missing documentation will not be considered submitted until the error is corrected.

(Agency) can revoke the approval of poll worker leave if operational circumstances of the employee’s work unit so necessitate, at the discretion of the employee’s immediate supervisor. A supervisor who makes this determination shall follow the existing procedures for revoking approved leave. It is the employee’s responsibility to notify the board of elections if the employee is no longer available to serve as a precinct election official on a particular Election Day.

An employee must verify to (Agency) the employee’s service as a precinct election official in each instance that the employee utilizes poll worker leave. On the workday following the election on which the employee returns to work, the employee shall deliver a properly completed and signed Poll Worker Leave Verification Form to the employee’s immediate supervisor. Additionally, within five working days of receiving payment from the local board of elections, the employee shall submit proof of the payment to the employee’s immediate supervisor. The supervisor shall attach the copy of the proof of payment to the Manager/Supervisor copy of the employee’s previously submitted request for leave form. Verification will not be considered timely if submitted more than six months beyond the date the employee utilized poll worker leave. Failure of the employee to timely submit the required verification discussed above to the employee’s immediate supervisor will cause the employee to be considered absent without leave (AWOL) for that day and may subject the employee to discipline, up to and including removal.

Any employee who fails to follow the procedures set forth in this work rule may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including removal. (Agency) will notify the Director of Administrative Services of any circumstances where fraud is suspected so that improper payments can be recovered.
