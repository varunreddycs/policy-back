                    2100-02 Mobile Computing


DAS Policy

No: 2100-02

Effective:
 October 27, 2025

Approval:


Katrina Flory, Assistant Director/State CIO

Purpose

This policy addresses the use, management, and control of Mobile Computing Devices used to connect to IT Resources owned, managed, or operated by the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). All defined terms are capitalized and a link to the glossary of definitions is found in section IV.

Scope

This policy applies to all DAS Organizational Users using State-Owned or Privately-Owned equipment to access DAS' IT Resources. This policy does not apply to Mobile Computing Devices or access methods used by the general public to access government services electronically.

Policy

This policy establishes minimum requirements for Mobile Computing Devices to protect State Data and DAS' IT Resources.

DAS Mobile Computing Device Management

DAS must establish standards and procedures for the acquisition, registration, use, and support of DAS-Owned Mobile Computing Devices.

DAS is under no obligation to provide support for the use of Privately-Owned devices outside of supporting the DAS-provided Mobile Device Management and Multifactor Authentication tools.

If a Mobile Computing Device is shared among multiple Organizational Users, for the purposes of tracking the device, DAS must maintain a single log to document the responsible User and the dates and times the device was checked in and out.

DAS retains ownership of any Mobile Computing Device purchased using DAS funds or otherwise acquired by DAS.

Lost or Stolen Mobile Computing Devices

DAS requires immediate reporting for lost or stolen Mobile Computing Devices. This includes State-Owned or Privately-Owned devices that are authorized for work use with systems or networks owned or managed by DAS. Organizational Users must report lost or stolen Mobile Computing Devices by contacting the DAS Customer Service Center (CSC) by phone at 877-644-6860 or by email at
csc@ohio.gov
.

Appropriate Use Requirements

DAS-Owned and Privately-Owned devices are subject to DAS and statewide policies concerning access to and use of state IT Resources and other state assets (refer to
Ohio Administrative Policy IT-04/Use of Internet, E-mail and Other IT Resources
 and
DAS Policy 700-01/Information Technology Resource Usage
).

State Data Considerations

Organizational Users must comply with the following requirements related to the protection of State Data.

When accessing State Data from a Privately-Owned Device, Organizational Users are prohibited from downloading Confidential Data (refer to State of Ohio Administrative Policy IT-04 and DAS Policy 700-01 for additional information).

Organizational Users are prohibited from accessing Federal Tax Information (FTI), Criminal Justice Information System (CJIS) Data, or other regulated Data from Privately-Owned Devices.

Organizational Users who conduct state work on a Privately Owned device must ensure that Non-Organizational Users do not access State Data on that device.

Organizational Users may be required to provide copies of State Data that may exist on Privately-Owned devices that are used to conduct state work. Privately-Owned devices used solely for the purposes of Multifactor Authentication are not, for the purposes of this clause, considered used for state work.

DAS is not liable for the safeguarding or maintenance of non-State Data on Privately-Owned Mobile Computing Devices used in support of official state business or otherwise while performing work on behalf of the state.

Procedures

The following are minimum requirements.

This policy must be distributed to each newly hired DAS Employee during orientation. The new DAS Employee must sign an acknowledgement of receipt of this policy prior to gaining access to IT Resources.

DAS Contractors must receive a copy of and sign an acknowledgement of receipt of this policy prior to gaining access to IT Resources.

Definitions

All defined terms are capitalized within the policy and the online glossary of definitions can be found at
IT Policy Glossary - Department of Administrative Services
.
