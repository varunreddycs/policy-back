No: VF-04 
Office of Fleet Management 
State of Ohio 
Administrative Policy 
 
Fleet Telematics 
 
 
Effective: 
October 10, 2024 
Issued By: 
 
Kathleen C. Madden, Director 
 
 
I. Purpose 
To establish statewide requirements for the use of Telematics equipment and data associated with 
state fleet vehicles within the Department of Administrative Services’ (DAS) Managed Fleet Program. 
The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy body is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to 
the definition in Section IV. 
 
II. Scope 
The Managed Fleet Program is provided by DAS’s Office of Fleet Management (OFM) to participating 
state agencies. This policy applies to all employees operating a state fleet vehicle that is within the 
Managed Fleet Program. 
III. Policy 
The Telematics equipment is used to monitor state fleet vehicle location and performance, which is 
intended to improve employee safety, operational efficiency, energy efficiency, and reduce costs. 
Use of Telematics data is intended to increase productivity and efficiency by tracking dispatching, 
routing, vehicle utilization, fuel economy, and preventative maintenance. 
 
A. Information Collection. Data obtained from Telematics is a valuable tool for operations and 
customer service that helps the State improve employee safety and extend the useful life and 
quality of the State’s fleet. Telematics collects information about state fleet vehicles such as 
location, speed, route, stopping, maneuvering severity, idle and parking time, and impact/crash 
notification. The State of Ohio uses this information to benefit the State and the customers it 
serves.

B. Designated Public Service Workers . Upon written request by an agency to OFM, state fleet 
vehicles within the Managed Fleet Program that are utilized by Designated  Public Service 
Workers may be equipped with a Telematics system that collects only engine diagnostic and 
basic utilization data. OFM is responsible for determining whether an employee is a Designated 
Public Service Worker for purposes of this section. 
 
C. No Expectation of Privacy. Employees have no expectation of privacy while using a state fleet 
vehicle. The state reserves the right to use Telematics data to monitor state fleet vehicles. 
 
D. Agency Responsibilities 
 
1. Agencies are responsible for informing employees that all vehicle operation is monitored by 
Telematics and providing a copy of this policy, as well as any agency- specific policy, to an 
employee who drives a state fleet vehicle. State agencies are permitted to issue additional 
policies and guidance on Telematics that expand on this policy where necessary to meet 
their unique needs. 
2. Agencies are responsible for enforcing compliance with this policy, the statewide Motor  
Vehicle Fleet Policy (VF-01), and any agency-specific guidance authorizing the use of state 
vehicles. 
3. Each state agency’s Telematics data and driver performance review process shall include 
the agency Fleet Coordinators, agency leadership and designated managers, and agency 
human resources representatives. 
4. Each agency will review its fleet Telematics data to ensure drivers eliminate unsafe and 
unauthorized driving behaviors. Regular monitoring of Telematics data for individual 
agency fleet programs is the responsibility of agency Fleet Coordinators or the agency 
person who manages day-to-day fleet operations as assigned. 
5. Agencies are responsible for addressing motor vehicle violations through appropriate 
agency policies and procedures. 
 
E. Use of DAS Motor Pool:  Employees utilizing the DAS motor pool will acknowledge this policy 
during the vehicle reservation process. 
 
F. Prohibited Activities: The improper, careless, negligent, destructive, or unsafe use or operation 
of state fleet vehicles and equipment, as well as the failure to report such use or operation and 
the failure to report damage or repair needs, may result in loss of the ability to utilize a state fleet 
vehicle under the statewide Motor Vehicle Fleet Policy (VF-01), loss of insurance coverage under 
the statewide Self -Insured Vehicle Liability Program (VF -02), and corrective or disciplinary 
action, including termination. The following activities are also prohibited: 
 
1. Tampering with or altering the physical Telematics equipment in a state or agency fleet 
vehicle. 
2. Intentionally blocking or interfering with the electrical systems, wireless signal, or satellite 
receiver/antenna. 
3. Attempting to repair or adjust the Telematics system in an agency state or agency fleet 
vehicle.

Employees who believe that the Telematics system in any fleet vehicle is not working properly 
should report the malfunction immediately to their agency’s Fleet Coordinator. 
G. Public Records Requests 
 
1. Public records requests received by DAS for records containing individualized Telematics 
data shall be sent to the applicable agency Fleet Coordinator for processing and response 
pursuant to that agency’s public records policy. 
2. Public records requests received by an agency participating in the DAS Managed Fleet 
Program for records containing engine diagnostic and basic utilization Telematics data 
shall be sent to the DAS Fleet Manager for processing and response pursuant to DAS’ public 
records policy. 
IV. Definitions 
 
A. Telematics. Technology that provides global positioning data, vehicle diagnostic data, and 
vehicle usage data to be used to improve fleet management costs, sustainability, efficiency, and 
safety. 
B. Designated Public Service Worker.  Those positions listed in Section 149.43(A)(7) of the Ohio 
Revised Code, and those positions that an agency demonstrates to OFM would be subject to a 
safety risk to themselves or others if their assigned state fleet vehicle were equipped with GPS 
monitoring.

ATTACHMENT 1: Model Agency Policy on Use of DAS and Agency Vehicles 
 
I. Purpose 
This policy establishes requirements for the use and safe operation of state vehicles and provides 
notice to [Agency] employees about the utilization of Telematics equipment in fleet and motor 
pool vehicles (Vehicles). The first occurrence of the word in the policy is in bold, italic type. 
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to all [Agency] employees who use a Vehicle. If any provision of this policy 
conflicts with the terms of an applicable collective bargaining agreement, the terms of the 
collection bargaining agreement prevail. 
III. Policy 
 
A. Authorized and Unauthorized Use: When using a Vehicle, employees are required to abide 
by State of Ohio Administrative Policy VF -01, Employee’s Use of Employer Provided Vehicles 
and State of Ohio Administrative Policy ORM-02, State Self-Insured Motor Vehicle Liability and 
Self-Insured Vehicle Damage Program. 
 
B. No Expectation of Privacy: Employees have no expectation of privacy while using a Vehicle. 
[Agency] reserves the right to use Telematics data to monitor Vehicles. 
 
C. Prohibited Activities: The improper, careless, negligent, destructive, or unsafe operation of 
Vehicles and equipment, as well as the failure to report such use or operation and the failure 
to report damage or repair needs, may result in loss of the ability to utilize a Vehicle under 
State of Ohio Administrative Policy VF -01, loss of insurance coverage under State of Ohio 
Administrative Policy ORM-02, and corrective or disciplinary action, including termination. 
 
Additionally, the following activities are prohibited: 
1. Tampering with or altering the physical Telematics equipment in a Vehicle. 
2. Intentionally blocking or interfering with the electrical systems, wireless signal, or 
satellite receiver/antenna in a Vehicle. 
3. Attempting to repair or adjust the Telematics system in Vehicle. 
 
Employees who believe that the Telematics system in a Vehicle is not working properly should 
report the malfunction immediately to the [Agency] Fleet Coordinator. 
 
IV. Definitions 
 
Telematics. Technology that provides global positioning data, vehicle diagnostic data, and vehicle 
usage data, and which is used to monitor DAS Vehicle location and performance and is intended 
to improve employee safety, operational efficiency, energy efficiency, and reduce costs.
