August 31, 2023

 |

Agency

State of Ohio Administrative Policy

No: HR-09 -
State Human Resources Division

Effective:
August 31, 2023

Issued By:
Kathleen C. Madden, Director

Purpose

This policy establishes the procedure appointing authorities must follow to request approval from the Director of the Department of Administrative Services (DAS), in coordination with the Director of the Office of Budget and Management (OBM), to grant employees who are exempt from collective bargaining (Exempt Employees) certain payments or benefits provided to employees subject to collective bargaining agreements. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type.

Scope

This policy only applies to agencies, boards, commissions, and elected officials whose employees are paid directly by warrant of the Director of the Office of Budget and Management (collectively referred to as Appointing Authority(ies)).

Policy

Payments or Benefits Subject to Parity Requests

Pursuant to Ohio Revised Code (ORC) 124.15(D), the Director of DAS may review collective bargaining agreements to determine whether certain payments or benefits provided to bargaining unit employees should be extended to Exempt Employees. Examples of such payments or benefits include, but are not limited to bereavement leave, personal leave, vacation leave, administrative leave, sick leave, disability benefits, holiday pay, and pay supplements. The Director of DAS and the Director of OBM may approve such payments or benefits to some or all Exempt Employees who are employed by an Appointing Authority with bargaining unit members for whom the payments or benefits exist. The payment or benefit cannot exceed the highest level for the payment or benefit specified in a collective bargaining agreement. A request to extend payments or benefits pursuant to this section may be initiated by an Appointing Authority or the Director of DAS.

Parity Request Procedures

An Appointing Authority wishing to extend payments or benefits provided in a collective bargaining agreement to certain Exempt Employees shall submit a written request to the Director of DAS and the Director of OBM.

The request shall include the following information:

A description of the payment or benefit to be extended;

A reference to the relevant article(s) of the applicable collective bargaining agreement for agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor. For separately elected officials, a copy of the relevant articles of the applicable collective bargaining agreement;

A listing of the classifications, OH|ID Workforce User ID (OH|ID), and Position Numbers for all Exempt Employees for whom the payments or benefits will be extended. This information shall be submitted electronically on an Excel spreadsheet;

An explanation of whether the Exempt Employees (i) are in a position with similar duties as bargaining unit employees receiving the payment or benefit, (ii) supervise bargaining unit employees receiving the payment or benefit, or (iii) are employed by the same Appointing Authority as the bargaining unit employees receiving the payment or benefit;

An explanation regarding whether the payment or benefit will be provided (i) at the same level paid to bargaining unit employees, or (ii) at a lower level than that paid to bargaining unit employees;

A cost analysis that (i) shows the additional costs the Appointing Authority will incur to extend the payment or benefit to Exempt Employees, and (ii) identifies the fund(s) from which such payment or benefit will be provided within current available appropriation levels;

A reference to any applicable law or rule that provides Exempt Employees with a similar payment or benefit, if applicable;

The duration of the applicable collective bargaining agreement;

The name, title, telephone number, and email address of the individual who is to be contacted if there are questions regarding the Appointing Authority’s written proposal.

In consultation with the Director of OBM, the Director of DAS will notify the Appointing Authority if the request is approved or denied by OBM and DAS. If a request is approved, the Director of DAS will establish the date upon which the payment or benefit will become effective and the expiration date.

Requests shall be approved only for a specific period of time. The standard duration will be tied to either the duration of the collective bargaining agreement in effect at the time the request is made or the biennium in which the approval is granted unless the Appointing Authority requests a shorter duration.

If an Appointing Authority wants to renew the parity benefit, a new request must be submitted pursuant to paragraph III.B. of this policy. To avoid interruption of the parity benefit, Appointing Authorities should renew the request either within 30 days of a successor collective bargaining agreement becoming effective or prior to the specific date set forth in the approval notice, whichever is applicable.
