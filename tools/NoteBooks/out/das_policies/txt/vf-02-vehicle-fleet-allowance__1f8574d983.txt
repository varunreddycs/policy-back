I. Purpose 
The Office of Fleet Management (OFM), within the Department of Administrative Services (DAS)  
manages the statewide motor vehicle fleet. This policy establishes guidelines and procedures for 
vehicle allowances.  
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to those individuals approved to receive a vehicle allowance. 
 
III. Policy 
 
A. This policy provides that certain individuals are eligible to receive a vehicle allowance to 
secure or lease transportation for that person’s use in the scope of that person’s employment 
or official duties. The individuals eligible for the vehicle allowance are:  
 
1. Those department heads appointed by the Governor and listed in ORC Section 121.03. 
2. Those chairpersons of commissions where the chairperson is appointed by the Governor 
and the appointment status is designated and compensated as full- time, permanent by 
the Director of Administrative Services.  
3. Other directors or department heads for which eligibility is approved by the Governor. 
B. Authorized Use of Motor Pool  Vehicles: Authorized individuals who receive the v ehicle 
allowance are generally prohibited from utilizing a state-owned or leased vehicle. Motor pool 
vehicles shall only be used for conducting state business  and in accordance with the

A motor pool vehicle will not be provided to an individual receiving a vehicle allowance that  
is for sole use by that individual . A motor pool vehicle may be used in the following 
circumstances while conducting state business:   
 
1. When there is an emergency  situation and it is  impractical to seek alternative 
transportation. 
2. When there are three (3) or more passengers  and it is more efficient to take one motor 
vehicle.  
3. When the situation/circumstance would require a specialized motor vehicle, i.e., off road 
capability. 
4. When driving out-of-state for business if it is the most economical mode of transportation. 
5. Any other circumstance approved in advance by the Director of DAS. 
C. Procedure 
 
1. Authorized individuals who wish to receive the vehicle allowance shall notify, in writing, 
the Director of DAS. The Director shall notify the individual  that the vehicle allowance is 
approved or disapproved, and a copy of the decision shall be provided to OFM.  
2. OFM shall annually review state payroll records to ensure that only authorized individuals 
are receiving the vehicle allowance. Any unauthorized use shall be reported by OFM to the 
Director of DAS.  
3. In consultation with the Director of the Office of Budget and Management, the Director of 
DAS may make changes to the vehicle allowance. If a change(s) is made, the Director of DAS 
shall inform the DAS State Human Resources Division and make any appropriate 
notifications.   
4. Payments for the vehicle allowance are processed through state payroll, appear on the 
employee’s second paycheck of each month, and are prorated based on effective date of 
approval by the Director of DAS and/or the termination from a position outlined in Section 
III.A.  
 
IV. Definitions 
 
None. 
 
V. Authority 
 
ORC Sections 124.14 and 125.832 
 
VI. Resources 
 
None.
