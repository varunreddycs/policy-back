         PROCEDURE 
  
Procedure Name: Offboarding Procedure 
Procedure No: P 450 
Issue Date: June 20, 2020 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
This offboarding procedure is necessary to ensure that a clearly defined process exists 
for collecting state-owned resources and removing access (electronic and physical) to 
systems and facilities in a timely and consistent manner, as a result of changes in the 
working status of personnel.  The exit procedure is fundamental to keeping up to date 
employment data, and maintaining the security of state personnel, data, systems, 
networks, assets, and facilities. 
 
 
II. SCOPE 
 
The scope of this procedure includes the steps that the employee, management and the 
Office of Employee Services (OES) shall take in order to recover state assets and 
remove access to state resources from personnel during transfer and termination 
events. 
 
Voluntary termination activities include, but are not limited to: 
 
• Resignation 
• Retirement 
• Transfer to a different DAS division 
• Transfer to other state agency, board, or commission 
 
Involuntary termination activities include, but are not limited to: 
 
• Unclassified Removal 
• Involuntary Disability Separation 
• Probationary Removal

The detailed actions that individual business or technical units perform internally to 
collect state assets and remove access to state resources are outside of the scope of 
this procedure. 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Voluntary Terminations 
 
Voluntary termination of employment occurs when an employee submits a 
written or verbal notice of resignation, retirement, transfer to another agency, 
to his or her supervisor. 
 
1. Employees are requested to provide a minimum of two weeks' notice of 
their intention to separate employment. The employee should provide a 
written resignation notification or intent to retire to his or her manager. 
Written notice of transfer to another agency or division is not required. 
Notification of transfers are obtained within OES. 
 
2. Upon receipt of an employee's resignation or retirement, the manager will 
notify the Office of Employee Services (OES) by sending a copy of the 
resignation or retirement letter. 
 
3. OES will obtain a Resignation Acceptance Letter from the HR 
Administrator and will send a copy to employee. If a retirement, OES will 
request Retirement Certificate from Governor’s Office. 
 
4. OES will send an Exit Survey to employee through Survey Monkey. Exit 
Survey is reviewed and a copy sent to Division Deputy Director, Section 
Administrator, and Assistant HR Administrator. Any negative remarks will 
be evaluated and managed. 
 
5. A New Employee, Separation & Transfer (NEST) ticket is initiated by OES 
in order to notify OIT of employee separation or transfer. 
 
6. Manager will complete the NEST ticket indicating what equipment to be 
recovered and accounts to be deactivated by OIT. 
 
7. Manager will submit OAKS HCM Online Security Request Form to disable 
employee’s access to MyOhio. 
 
8. OES will schedule and conduct an Exit Meeting with employee to cover 
leave payouts, ethics policies, COBRA, etc. 
 
9. OES will notify DAS Asset Group of separation so that all assets can be 
recovered and/or verified.

10. An electronic Personnel Action Request (ePAR) is created by OES and 
approved copy sent to DAS Finance Budget and Planning unit, OES 
Payroll Manager, and Asset Group. 
 
11. Manager completes Exit Interview Checklist on employee’s last day 
worked, and obtains employee’s ID badge and any other relevant items 
(e.g, keys, uniforms, cell phone, USB drives, documents). Checklist is 
submitted to OES along with ID badge. Cell phones and laptops are 
submitted to OIT. 
 
12. OES submits ID badge to Security Services for deactivation. 
 
13. Table of Organization is updated by OES. 
 
14. All separation documentation is uploaded to OnBase for file maintenance. 
 
B. Involuntary Terminations 
 
An involuntary termination of employment is a management-initiated 
dismissal. 
 
1. Appropriate documentation on the involuntary termination is obtained by 
OES. 
 
2. OES will conduct an Exit Meeting with employee (either in-person or 
remotely) to cover leave payouts, COBRA, etc. 
 
3. OES will complete Exit Interview Checklist with employee and obtain 
employee’s ID badge and any other relevant items (e.g, ID badge, keys, 
uniforms, cell phone, USB drives). Cell phones and laptops are submitted 
to OIT. If employee is not in the office at the time of separation, OES will 
make arrangements with employee to drop off needed items on a date 
agreed to by OES and the employee. 
 
4. OES will escort employee to obtain personal items from their office or will 
gather these items for pick-up by the employee at a later date. 
 
5. A NEST ticket is initiated by OES in order to notify OIT of employee 
separation. 
 
6. Manager will complete the NEST ticket indicating what equipment to be 
recovered and accounts to be deactivated by OIT. 
 
7. Manager will submit OAKS HCM Online Security Request Form to disable 
employee’s access to MyOhio.

8. OES will notify DAS Asset Group of separation so that all assets can be 
recovered and/or verified. 
 
9. An electronic Personnel Action Request (ePAR) is created by OES and 
approved copy sent to DAS Finance Budget and Planning unit, OES 
Payroll Manager, and Asset Group. 
 
10. OES submits ID badge to Security Services for deactivation. 
 
11. Table of Organization is updated by OES. 
 
12. All separation documentation is uploaded to OnBase for file maintenance.
