I. Purpose  
This policy establishes requirements for purchasing supplies and services as a result of  either an 
Emergency or a State Procurement Emergency. The first occurrence of a defined term in this policy is 
in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV. 
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to State Agencies superintended with purchasing authority by the Department of  
Administrative Services (DAS).  
 
III. Policy  
State Agencies shall limit purchases to only those items necessary for Response and Recovery activities 
during an Emergency or a State Procurement Emergency. This policy is not for a Public Exigency, which 
are managed by the Ohio Facilities Construction Commission (OFCC) in accordance with Ohio Revised 
Code (ORC) 123.10 and 123.11 . State Agencies having questions about or encountering a Public 
Exigency shall contact OFCC. 
 
A. Emergencies: During an Emergency, ORC 125.061(B) allows DAS to suspend the purchasing and 
contracting requirements of ORC Chapter 125 for the Ohio Emergency Management Agency (EMA) 
or any State Agency participating in Response and Recovery activities. Additionally, DAS can 
suspend any requirement of ORC Chapter 153 that would otherwise apply to the State Agency. 
 
1. DAS shall initiate this process when the Director of the Ohio Department of Public Safety (DPS) 
or the Executive Director of EMA request s a declaration of an Emergency from the Governor of 
the State of Ohio or the President of the United States.  
2. At the same time as this request, the Director of DPS or the Executive Director of EMA shall also 
send another request to the DAS Director and DAS Chief Procurement Officer (CPO) to suspend 
purchasing and contracting requirements. The request shall include this information:

B. State Procurement Emergencies: During a state procurement Emergency, ORC 125.061(C) allows 
DAS to suspend the purchasing and contracting requirements of ORC Chapter 125 for any State 
Agency. 
 
1. This process is initiated when either the director or the administrative head of the State Agency 
where the state procurement Emergency exists submits a request to DAS to suspend purchasing 
and contracting requirements.  
2. A request to suspend purchasing and contracting requirements shall be sent to the DAS Director 
and the DAS CPO. The request shall include this information:  
a) Details of the state procurement Emergency, including a description of its immediacy. 
b) A description of the item(s) that cannot be acquired through normal procurement methods 
in a timely manner. 
3. Purchases shall not be made until DAS reviews and approves the request in accordance with 
Section C of this policy. 
  
C. Reviewing Requests to Suspend Purchasing and Contracting Requirements: DAS is responsible 
for reviewing and making  determinations on  requests to suspend purchasing and contracting 
requirements due to either an Emergency or a State Procurement Emergency.  
 
1. Once a determination is made, DAS shall provide  written notice of the outcome to the 
requesting State Agency.  
2. When a suspension of purchasing and contracting requirements is approved, the DAS Director 
will notify both the Director of the Office of Budget and Management (OBM) and the members 
of the Controlling Board. 
3. Purchase requisitions shall be submitted through OhioBuys. 
a) State Agencies shall follow instructions on “How to Submit an Emergency Purchase 
Requisition,”(https://procure.ohio.gov/static/pdf/Agency%20Training/02.18%20Ohio%20
Buys%20Submit%20an%20Emergency%20Purchase%20Requistion%20JA%20v2.3.pdf).  
b) State Agencies shall obtain a release and permit from DAS and fulfill needs through normal 
procurement methods whenever practical. 
c) Emergency or State Procurement Emergency purchases must only be for the amount of 
time reasonably required for Response and Recovery activities.  
4. When purchasing with a payment card (Pcard), State Agencies shall refer to the State of Ohio 
Payment Card Policy  (http://finsource.ohio.gov/SAFEManual/index.htm#t=(SAFE)_State_of_ 
OhioPayment_Card_Program.htm).

IV. Definitions 
 
A. Emergency. Any period of time when an Emergency has been declared or proclaimed by either the 
Congress of the United States, the President of the United States, and/or the Governor of Ohio.  
B. Recovery. All those activities required and necessary to return an area to its former condition to the 
extent possible following the occurrence of any hazard. 
C. Response. All those activities that occur after any hazard to remedy the hazard and that provide 
Emergency assistance from the effects of any such hazard, reduce the probability of further injury, 
damage, or destruction, and are designed or undertaken to speed Recovery operations. 
D. Public Exigency . An injury or obstruction that has occurred, or there is an immediate danger of 
occurring, in any public works of the State that materially impairs the public work’s immediate use 
or jeopardizes property adjacent to it. 
E. State Agencies. Every organized body, office, or agency established by the laws of the State for the 
exercise of any function of State government. It does not include elected State officials, the General 
Assembly or any legislative agency, a court or any judicial agency , or a State institution of higher 
education.  
F. State Procurement Emergency . A situation that results from the following: (1) a threat to public 
health, safety, or welfare; and (2) a serious threat of harm to the functioning of State government, 
the preservation or protection of property, or the health or safety of any person and  therefore 
creates an immediate and serious need for supplies or services that cannot be met through normal 
procurement methods.
