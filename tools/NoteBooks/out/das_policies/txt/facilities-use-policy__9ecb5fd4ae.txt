I. Purpose 
To provide direction, active regulation , and control of all activities, decorations, and  displays 
permitted in facilities managed by the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). This policy 
affects all DAS managed facilities (the Facilities) including: 30 East Broad Street, Columbus, Ohio; 
77 South High Street, Columbus, Ohio; 246 N. High Street, Columbus, Ohio; 35 East Chestnut 
Street, Columbus, Ohio; 25 South Front Street, Columbus, Ohio; 4200 Surface Road, Columbus, 
Ohio; 1320 Arthur E. Adams Drive, Columbus, Ohio; 8895 E. Main Street, Reynoldsburg, Ohio; and 
615 W. Superior Avenue, Cleveland, Ohio. 
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to all tenants of the Facilities and to all members of the public who enter the 
Facilities. This policy does not apply to any activities conducted by DAS in the Facilities.   
 
III. Policy 
The Facilities are places of business for its tenants and members of the public with business with 
the Facility tenants. This Policy is designed to ensure the safety of all Facility tenants and guests, 
to provide all Facility tenants and guests with orderly access to the Facilities , and to keep the 
Facilities clean and professional in appearance.

B. Proposed Use of The Lobby or Public Reception Areas 
 
1. In limited instances, the lobby and public reception areas of the Facilities may be used 
by Facility tenants for pre-approved activities that benefit State of Ohio employees. 
Facility tenants are required to submit a formal request to the DAS Facility Manager 
for any proposed use of the lobby or public reception areas  of the Facilities (See 
Section VII).  
2. A request shall include the name and title of the person completing the application, 
the proposed activities, the purpose of the proposed activities, the proposed date(s) 
and times of the planned activities, the proposed set-up, and the expected number of 
people involved.  
3. The Facility Manager may deny a request for any reason  and may cancel a request 
even after the applicant has begun using the Facility.  
 
C. Prohibited Activities . The following activities are prohibited in the lobbies or public 
reception areas of the Facilities:  
 
1. Private social activities, regardless of type or content. 
2. Serving or consuming alcoholic beverages. 
3. Serving or distributing food, food products, or non-alcoholic beverages regardless of 
the nature or reason for the distribution , unless approved pursuant to a request 
submitted under Section III.B. 
4. Solicitation of any kind, including the solicitation of contributions, gifts or donations, 
the solicitation of signatures on petitions, and the solicitation of support for any 
political candidate or cause.  This rule shall not prevent solicitation in connection with 
State of Ohio charitable fundraisers or events. 
5. Distributing pamphlets, brochures, or printed materials other than in connection with 
activities approved pursuant to Section III.B.  
6. Any activity that obstructs the free ingress or egress of those wishing to enter or leave 
the Facility.  
7. A sound level, noise, or any other activity that disrupts the business of government in 
the public building.
