                  Advanced Step Appointments (HR-03)


August 31, 2023

 |

Agency

State of Ohio Administrative Policy

No: HR-03 -
State Human Resources Division

Effective:
August 31, 2023

Issued By:
Kathleen C. Madden, Director

Purpose

To establish uniform procedures and guidelines for requesting advanced step appointments. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV.

Scope

This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).

Policy

It is the policy of the State of Ohio to permit advanced step appointments pursuant to section 124.15(E) of the Ohio Revised Code in a fair and consistent manner. Agencies seeking to hire a new employee, or to promote or accept the transfer of an existing state employee, at an advanced step, must conduct an analysis and submit a request as established in the sections below. Approval of advanced step requests is at the discretion of the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). There are only two circumstances where an advanced step appointment may be authorized:
Exceptional Qualifications
 and
Serious Labor Market Conditions
.

Agency Analysis and Standards of Review

Before considering an advanced step, the Agency must conduct an analysis according to the applicable standard provided below.

Standard for Exceptional Qualifications: For an employee’s qualifications to be sufficiently exceptional for purposes of this policy, the qualifications must exceed the minimum qualifications (minimum educational and/or work experience) of the position.

Work-related experience may be substituted for core program course work in those positions where no education is required.

Agencies should use Attachment 1 as the tool for determining equivalences that demonstrate the candidate meets at least the minimum qualifications.

An advanced degree or work-related experience exceeding the minimum education requirements may support an appointment to a higher step.

Standard for Serious Market Conditions: For labor market conditions to be sufficiently serious for the purpose of this policy, there must be a critical labor shortage in the classification being filled which has led to recruitment difficulties. Agencies must gather and analyze all the following information in their analysis:

The geographic area where the Agency is experiencing recruitment difficulties. This area could be statewide, locally, regionally, etc.

Within the geographic area only, identify the other government agencies and/or private employers the Agency is competing with for talent and compare the salaries of employees doing comparable work.

To help ensure the work is comparable, the Agency will need to consider whether a certification or licensure is required for the position, if certain skills or specialties are needed, the required education/experience needed, and whether those criteria are comparable to the position the Agency is trying to fill.

The number of qualified applicants who applied and the number of qualified applicants who declined offers due to salary.

Identify the impact to incumbents of positions in the same classification (e.g., will the advanced step result in wage compression between supervisor and subordinate).

Any survey data.

Justification Procedure

All advanced step appointments must include the following information and documentation when submitted through the electronic personnel action request (ePAR) system.

Exceptional Qualifications: Advanced steps based on the employee’s Exceptional Qualifications must include all the following:

A letter from the Agency that provides:

The name of the applicant to be hired;

The position number and classification of the applicant to be hired;

The step of the pay range being requested;

A brief explanation of the reason for the advanced step, including an explanation of the Agency’s analysis pursuant to the standard of review outlined in Section III.A.1. of this policy.

A copy of the applicant’s resume or state job application.

A copy of the applicable position description.

Serious Labor Market Conditions: Advanced steps based on Serious Labor Market Conditions must include a letter from the Agency that provides:

The name of the applicant to be hired;

The position number and classification of the applicant to be hired;

The step of the pay range being requested;

A brief explanation of the reason for the advanced step, including an explanation of the Agency’s analysis pursuant to the standard of review outlined in Section III.A.2. of this policy; and

The labor market data and information gathered in accordance with Section III.A.2 of this policy.

Implementation

Appointing Authority Requirements: All appointing authorities are responsible for implementation of advanced step hiring in accordance with the Revised Code and this policy. For bargaining unit employees, Agencies must follow any applicable collective bargaining agreements or applicable Letters of Agreement.

DAS Requirements: The DAS State Human Resources Division will be responsible for reviewing ePARs for advanced step appointments at least once a quarter to ensure Agency compliance with the Revised Code and this policy.

Definitions

Exceptional Qualifications:
 The candidate/employee (“employee”) has qualifications that exceed the minimum qualifications required for the position and such qualifications are determined to be exceptional by the director of DAS.

Serious Labor Market Conditions:
 There is a critical labor market shortage in the classification being filled, which makes it extremely difficult to recruit employees at the minimum rate for the classification.

Standard for Exceptional Qualifications

For a candidate’s qualifications to be sufficiently exceptional for purposes of this policy, the qualifications must exceed the minimum qualifications of the position either through education or experience or a combination thereof. Agencies should use the above Minimum Qualifications Conversion Chart as the tool for determining equivalencies demonstrating that the candidate meets at least the minimum qualifications.

Calculation of Advanced Step for Exceptional Qualifications

Step one is the beginning step of any pay grade. Candidates may be offered an advanced step for each year of experience or equivalency that exceeds the minimum qualifications of the classification.
