I. Purpose 
The Office of Fleet Management (OFM), within the Department of Administrative Services (DAS),  
supports State Agencies reducing fuel waste and the public’s exposure to State Vehicle exhaust 
by limiting unnecessary Idling in State Vehicles. The purpose of this policy is to reduce the 
petroleum consumed and pollution emitted by State Vehicles and to improve air quality.  The first 
occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition 
in Section IV.  
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to all Drivers of State Vehicles.  
 
III. Policy 
 
A. Responsibilities: No Driver who operates a State Vehicle designed to operate on public roads 
shall allow a gasoline-powered or diesel -powered State Vehicle to Idle in excess of  ten (10) 
consecutive minutes. No State Vehicle shall be left running and parked/unattended for any 
reason, except in the following situations: 
 
1. The State Vehicle is forced to remain motionless on a public road because of traffic 
conditions over which the Driver has no control. 
2. The State Vehicle is being used as an emergency vehicle in an emergency situation. 
3. The State Vehicle is used for security that is required to idle at institutions.  
4. The State Vehicle is used to provide heating/cooling to ensure the safety and health of the 
K-9 units.

powered State Vehicles and Idling of the State Vehicle is necessary to ensure the safety or 
health of the passengers or Driver. 
 
IV. Definitions 
 
A. State Agency . Every organized body, office, board, authority, commission, or agency 
established by the laws of the state for the exercise of any governmental or quasi -
governmental function of state government regardless of the funding source for that entity, 
other than any st ate institution of higher education, the office of the governor, lieutenant 
governor, auditor of state, treasurer of state, secretary of state, or attorney general, the general 
assembly or any legislative agency, the courts or any judicial agency, or any s tate retirement 
system or retirement program established by or referenced in the Revised Code. 
B. Driver. Any employee of a State Agency, inmate, volunteer, or other Authorized Operator of a 
State Vehicle.  
C. State Vehicle . Includes automobiles, trucks, motor vehicles with auxiliary equipment, self -
propelling equipment or trailers, aircraft, or watercraft, that are owned by a State Agency.  
D. Idling. Idling is a vehicle condition in which the engine will run, but the vehicle is not in motion. 
The vehicle’s idling speed is set to be sufficient to keep the engine running but not the 
vehicle. When idling, the engine runs without any loads except the engine accessories, and 
without additional fuel via the gas pedal. 
E. Authorized Operator.  Person approved or assigned by the State Agency to perform a specific 
type of duty or function.
