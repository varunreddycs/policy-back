Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to provide information on changing the civil service status of positions. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV.

Scope

This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).

Policy

Agencies are required to follow the steps outlined below in order to change the civil service status of a filled position.

Agency Submission to DAS

Required Documents: An Agency seeking to change the civil service status of a filled position must submit the documentation listed in the Civil Service Status Change Checklist via email to the Ohio Department of Administrative Services (DAS), Office of Collective Bargaining (OCB), Labor Relations and Human Resources Policy section at
DASHRD.HRPolicy@das.ohio.gov
.

ePar

Simultaneous to the document submission outlined above, the Agency must submit a Data Change/Status–Civil Service ePar. The ePar will not be processed until OCB receives, reviews, and approves the documentation, at which time the ePar will be routed back to the requesting Agency through the ePar system.

If an approved ePar will change a position's civil service designation to unclassified, after the ePar has been routed back to the requesting Agency, the employee holding the position must sign the required unclassified acknowledgement form, indicating the employee understands that the Agency changed the civil service designation for the position to unclassified. Decentralized Agencies shall attach the acknowledgement when the Agency enters the final approval in the ePar system. Centralized Agencies shall attach the acknowledgement when the Agency enters the final Agency-level approval prior to the ePar being routed to DAS SHRD State Services.

Legal Review

Agencies should consult with their legal counsel with respect to any civil service status questions.

Civil Service Status Change Checklist

Agencies must submit the following to OCB:

Request/Justification letter that includes a summary of the changed job duties and any relevant Ohio Revised Code section.

Signed and dated copy of the current position description.

Signed and dated copy of the new position description.

Current table of organization. This table of organization must allow for the tracking of the position in question up the organization’s structure to the Agency Director.

Proposed table of organization. This table of organization must allow for the tracking of the position in question up the organization’s structure to the Agency Director.

List of employees in the Agency in the same classification and their civil service status, noting any changes in civil service status in the past four years.

If you have any questions, please contact the DAS OCB Labor Relations and Human Resources Policy section at
DASHRD.HRPolicy@das.ohio.gov
 or 614.752.5393.

Unclassified Service Explanation and Acknowledgment (Read Only Form)

I,
Name:


, acknowledge the following:

Employees in the unclassified civil service of the State of Ohio do not have a property interest in their positions.

Employees in the unclassified civil service of the State of Ohio will never gain a property interest in their unclassified positions regardless of the amount of time they remain in their unclassified positions.

Employees in the unclassified civil service of the State of Ohio serve at the pleasure of the appointing authority and may be removed from their unclassified position at any time and for any reason that is not unlawful.

Employees who are removed from positions in the unclassified civil service of the State of Ohio do not have appeal rights to the State Personnel Board of Review.
