Subject:  Subscriber Equipment Update, June 2021 
 
Dear MARCS Subscriber,  
 
As part of ongoing efforts to ensure the Ohio Multi-Agency Radio Communication 
System (MARCS) meets the latest technology needs of subscribers and the citizens 
they serve, the MARCS Steering Committee recently approved updates to policy MPP 
31.1; Minimum Requirements for P25 Compliance on the MARCS Statewide Public 
Safety Communication System. 
 
Included in this update is a method allowing MARCS to structure system keys to the 
many partners across Ohio.  MARCS system keys submitted for renewal will no longer 
support end of life (EOL) devices effective January 1, 2022.  MARCS staff will 
maintain system keys for EOL devices. 
 
Radio Authentication (RA) is being required by July 1, 2025.  If you have EOL devices 
that are capable of being updated to RA, these radios can continue to be used even 
past the July 1, 2025 deadline.  RA, coupled with hard system keys no longer 
supporting EOL devices, will alleviate the security risk associated with pirated soft 
system keys. 
 
Beginning July 1, 2022, MARCS will no longer activate new device requests unless 
the device has Radio Authentication, and MARCS will no longer allow the transfer of 
EOL devices between agencies unless the device has Radio Authentication.  MARCS 
will monitor incoming requests for service.  If there is a question with an EOL device, 
MARCS staff will work directly with the agencies involved. 
 
An up-to-date approved subscriber equipment list is available on the MARCS 
webpage at https://tinyurl.com/4nxu6tc8.  Please evaluate your devices to ensure they 
meet the minimum requirements outlined in MPP 31.1. 
 
The use of manufacturer-supported equipment is essential to safe and secure public 
safety communications.  We appreciate your partnership and support as we work to 
maintain a secure MARCS environment. 
 
For more information, please contact the MARCS Program Office by sending an e-
mail to DAS-MARCS.Admin@das.ohio.gov.   
 
Sincerely, 
 
 Rick Schmahl   
MARCS Program Director

Subject:  Radio Authentication Update, June 2021 
 
Dear MARCS Subscriber, 
 
As part of efforts by the Multi-Agency Radio Communication System (MARCS) 
Steering Committee to upgrade technology, improve reliability of the MARCS system, 
and protect the safety of Ohio first responders, all radios using the system will be 
required to have Radio Authentication (RA) by July 2025.  
 
The RA standard, approved by the Steering Committee during its December meeting as 
part of updates to MARCS policy MPP 31.1; Minimum Requirements for P25 
Compliance on the MARCS Statewide Public Safety Communication System 
(https://tinyurl.com/yymvv6ub), will protect Ohio first responders by eliminating 
unauthorized radios from accessing the system.   
 
The requirement for all new radio purchases after July 1, 2019 to include the P25 
authentication feature was first addressed during the April 2019 MARCS radio shop 
meeting attended by nearly 70 members of the radio manufacturing, vendor, and 
support communities. At the time, we noted that any radios purchased after July 1, 
2019 may have RA without your knowledge. While the deadline for P25 Radio 
Authentication is July 1, 2025, radios purchased after July 1, 2019 may already have 
authentication that can be enabled now. 
 
MARCS will share the cost of RA with your agency to ensure security and 
communications reliability by funding all system core licenses, including recurring 
costs for providing a dedicated circuit enabling approved keyloaders to update the 
system remotely via a VPN over this dedicated circuit. However, subscriber device 
licenses are the responsibility of the owning agency/department.   
 
MARCS is actively managing system license requirements with vendors to remain 
informed of radio counts for agencies purchasing the licenses or new radios with RA to 
ensure enough licenses are in the system. With several years until RA becomes 
mandatory, it is our hope that subscribers will be able to spread costs over budgeting 
periods while allowing MARCS to do the same as radios with RA come on to the 
system. 
 
MARCS has been working with its vendors and making clear the need for consistent 
and best pricing. The discussions were well received, and all understand the economies 
of scale at play due to the number of radios on the system.

1.0 Purpose 
 
The purpose of this document is to define the rules and regulations concerning the eligibility, 
security, distribution, and utilization of APCO P -25 compliant radio devices on the MARCS 
Statewide Public Safety Communication Network. 
 
2.0 Scope 
 
This policy, and the rules and regulations set forth herein, apply universally for all radio devices 
sold/purchased/transferred for use on the MARCS Statewide Public Safety Communication 
System. 
 
3.0 Background 
 
The MARCS Statewide Public Safety Communication Network is a Motorola VoIP, Project 25 
Phase 1 compliant public safety communication system.   Project 25 (P25 or APCO-25) is a suite 
of standards for digital radio communications for use by federal, state/province and local public 
safety agencies in North America , enabling radio devices from various manufacturers to 
communicate with each other for purposes of interoperability.  In order to remain P25 complaint, 
MARCS herein outlines minimum requirements for radio devices to be placed on the MARCS 
Statewide Public Safety Communication Network. 
 
4.0 Minimum Requirements 
 
4.1 All voice radio devices (e.g. - portable, mobile, remote-control station) shall operate on the 
MARCS P-25 digital trunked 700/800 MHz system, including seamless inter -zone 
roaming.  The roaming requirement includes affiliation into different zones upon activation 
of the radio from the “off” position.  
4.2 All devices shall adhere to the current published APCO P-25 standards.  
4.3 All devices shall operate on the 700 MHz/800 MHz frequency range.

4.4 All devices must support P25 Radio Authentication. 
4.5 Discontinued Manufacturers devices will not be permitted to be new devices activated on 
the MARCS system. This includes discontinued devices transferred from one agency to 
another. 
4.6 The device provider/manufacturer must provide a system security protocol (i.e. – system 
key) allowing MARCS and MARCS only to activate the devices on the MARCS Public 
Safety Communication Network. Soft system keys are not acceptable. 
4.7 The device provider/manufacturer must also notify MARCS personnel  of any software 
programming updates, or radio “flashes”, on a timely basis. 
4.8 Vendors must provide MARCS with 4 operational radio units from production, and permit 
MARCS to demonstrate the units’ capabilities as outlined above, prior to making the units 
available to MARCS subscribers.  This provision shall i nclude all necessary hardware, 
software, cabling, including standard Motorola Key-Loader cables for encryption loading, 
for all approved models of all approved subscribers, for each MARCS programmer to allow 
MARCS to program radios on an ongoing basis.  
 
5.0 Prohibitions 
 
Any unauthorized sharing or  copying of MARCS Public Safety Communication System data, 
including system key information, other security codes, hexadecimal identifiers and/or any related 
documentation; publicizing of the said data; or other unau thorized use of the MARCS  secure 
footprint is expressly prohibited. 
 
6.0 Enforcement of Violations 
 
Alleged violations of these rules shall result in a thorough administrative and/or criminal 
investigation by agents of MARCS and/or any authorized law enforcement agencies. 
 
Proven violations of these rules shall result in MARCS administrative disciplinary actions, up to 
and including restriction or termination of MARCS participation, and/or prosecution pursuant to 
the appropriate charges found in the Ohio Revised Code. 
 
7.0 Effective Date 
 
The current version of this policy was approved and adopted by the MARCS Steering Committee 
on May 9, 2023.
