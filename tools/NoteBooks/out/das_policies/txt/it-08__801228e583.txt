I. Purpose 
The purpose of this policy is to establish website standardization, content management, and 
security requirements for Agency websites. The intent of these requirements is to create a degree 
of consistency and predictability among Public -facing Web Page s, while still providing the 
flexibility to design secure web pages that fit the unique needs of Agencies. The first occurrence of 
a defined term in the policy body outside of a title or heading is in bold, italic type, and is 
hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV. 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the 
Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies). The majority of the requirements 
outlined within this policy apply only to Public-facing Web Pages. However, the following sections 
apply to Public-facing Web Pages as well as Agency web-facing applications: Section III.K. Browser 
Compatibility, Section II I.L. Transport Layer Security  (TLS) Certificates, Section III.P. 
Identification and Authentication, and Section  III.Q. Website and Web -Facing Application 
Security. 
 
In accordance with Ohio Revised Code (ORC) Section 125.183, the requirements related to Covered 
Applications outlined in Section III.C. of this policy apply to state agencies, which are defined as  
every organized body, office, or agency established by the laws of this state for the exercise of any

III. Policy 
Agencies shall align with the website design and content management standards defined by the  
InnovateOhio Platform  team when developing and maintaining Public -facing Web Page s on 
State-controlled Websites. 
 
A. User Experience Digital Toolkit : Agencies shall utilize the InnovateOhio Platform Use r 
Experience Digital Toolkit  to design their public facing web pages. The toolkit includes the 
following: 
1. Style Guide (e.g., accessibility, color palette, typography, dependencies, etc.) 
2. User Interface Controls (e.g., ads grid, accordions, arrows and popovers, badges, 
breadcrumbs, buttons, card carousel, checkboxes, labels, left and main navigation, 
maps, etc.)  
3. Content Components (e.g., charts, icons, lists, tables, etc.) 
4. Page Examples (e.g., form, landing, login, password reset, etc.) 
5. Layout (e.g., cards, dividers, form, grid system, etc.) 
6. Page Errors (e.g., 404-page, 500 page, etc.) 
 
B. Branding: Each Agency shall have both Agency and Ohio.gov branding. This branding shall be 
consistent on all Agency pages. 
1. Branding shall comply with the style in effect on the Ohio.gov home page. 
2. Agencies are permitted to use their own logos and branding. Agencies with subordinate 
Agencies shall use the subordinate Agency brand in addition to a link to the Agency home 
page. 
3. Agency branding shall be linked to the home page of the Agency’s website. Subordinate 
Agency branding shall link to the subordinate Agency home page. 
 
 
C. Social Media: The use of Social Media shall align with the following requirements:

2. The download and/or use of any unauthorized social media , including Covered

a) All Covered Applications must be immediately removed from all S tate-Owned or

b) Exceptions to the Covered Applications prohibition shall only be considered for Law 
Enforcement Purposes or Security Purposes  and must be authorized by the state

any proposed uses, the agency must document the measures that will be taken to 
mitigate against the security and privacy risks associated with the use of a Covered 
Application. Examples of possible mitigation strategies include: 
(i) Accessing the Covered Application from a secure device that does not connect 
to the state’s network or wireless services. 
(ii) Ensuring that the device that is used to access the Covered Application does not 
store or transmit Confidential Data. 
c) Any requests for exception shall be submitted to the Ohio Department of 
Administrative Services (DAS) Office of Information Security and Privacy (OISP) via 
the Security Exception Request  form. The form is located within the IT Enterprise 
Services Portal under the “Services & Products/Security Services” category. 
3. Agencies shall take measures to prevent the downloading , installation, or use of a 
Covered Application. 
4. Agencies shall monitor their use of Social Media  and State-Owned or leased devices to 
ensure compliance with this policy. 
5. Agencies’ Social Media content coordinators shall be reminded, at least annually, of the 
requirements regarding prohibited and appropriate use as outlined within Ohio 
Administrative Policy IT-04, “Use of Internet, E-mail and Other IT Resources.” 
6. Third party tracking from Social Media links, for example, social network sharing buttons, 
widgets or plug -ins, shall not be permitted unless explicitly approved by the Agency. If 
Social Media links are authorized by the Agency to track user activities, then this shall be 
disclosed in the website privacy statement. 
7. Links to Social Media  shall appear in the Social Media  column of the State Directory, 
https://ohio.gov/wps/portal/gov/site/help-center/state-directory/.

D. S earch Feature : Each Agency shall have a search feature available and when technically 
feasible, utilize the InnovateOhio Platform search engine. 
 
1. The search feature shall have the capability to perform searches within the Agency  
website. 
2. The search bar appearance and functionality shall be consistent across all Agency  web 
pages. 
 
E. Navigation: Each Agency shall provide consistent navigation on each web page. 
 
1. The primary navigation bar shall be implemented on all Agency web pages. 
2. The primary navigation bar shall be populated with Agency- specific links and shall 
remain consistent across all web pages of a division or department. 
3. Primary and secondary navigation shall be text-based; images or other formats shall not 
be used. 
4. All images with hyperlink capability shall include an alternative text (alt text) description 
that conveys the action that will be initiated when the user clicks on the link (e.g., 
destination). 
5. Navigation within self-contained or embedded content, such as interactive animations, 
video, or data visualizations, is permitted but should only be used within the context of 
the component. 
 
F. Content Area Design: The layout and organization of each web page content area shall 
remain primarily at the discretion of the Agency, department, or division; Agencies shall refer 
to the InnovateOhio Platform User Experience Digital Toolkit web page for content and user 
experience best practices. 
 
G. Website Content: Agencies shall comply with the following website content requirements.  
Each Agency shall maintain an accurate contact page containing the following information: 
 
1. A general phone number that is answered by a person during business hours. 
2. A media relations phone number (if available). 
3. A general phone number that is answered by a person during business hours. 
4. An e-mail address or submission form for general concerns.

H. Privacy Notice: Agency websites shall provide a hyperlink to a privacy notice on every web 
page. At a minimum, the privacy notice shall include the following elements: 
 
1. The privacy notice shall describe the types of personal information the Agency  collects 
through the website and why it is necessary. 
2. The privacy notice shall provide an explanation of how the user's personal information is 
collected, including directly, such as through web applications and online forms, and 
indirectly, such as through website usage analytical tools. 
3. The privacy notic e shall disclose how the Agency uses personal information collected 
through the website, including secondary uses and public record requirements. 
4. Agencies shall inform website users that they may have rights under ORC sections 149.43, 
1347.08, 1347.09, and 1347.15 with respect to inspecting, obtaining, correcting, and 
disputing personal information the Agency collects or maintains through the website. 
5. ORC Section 1347.15 requires each Agency to post on its’ website the policies adopted in 
accordance with that law. The privacy notice shall have a section labeled “Confidential 
Personal Information.” The section will include links to the Agency’s administrative rules, 
policies and procedures issued in accordance with ORC 1347.15. If an Agency  does not 
maintain any confidential personal information under ORC 1347.15, it shall include a 
statement to that fact instead. 
6. Agencies shall provide website users with an Agency  point of contact who addresses 
privacy questions. The contact information shall include the mailing address and at least 
one of the following: a telephone number, e-mail address or contact form. 
7. The privacy notice shall be kept up to date to accurately reflect Agency  information 
practices. The privacy notice shall display the latest revision date. 
8. Privacy notices shall be consistent with all applicable laws that regulate the collection, 
use and maintenance of personal information. 
9. Each Agency's privacy notice link shall be labeled as "privacy" or "privacy notice." (Refer 
to the footer on Ohio.gov for an example of labeling.)

J. Mobile Ready : Agencies shall utilize InnovateOhio Platform templates and components, 
available at ux.ohio.gov, to ensure a responsive user experience on any device (e.g., mobile 
phones, tablets). 
 
K. Browser Compatibility: Agency websites and web-facing applications shall render properly 
in the current, supported version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. 
 
L. Transport Layer Security (TLS) Certificates : All publicly accessible, State -controlled 
Websites and web-facing applications shall only provide information and services through a 
hypertext transfer protocol secure (https) connection, encrypted using TLS certificates.  
 
1. TLS certificates may be obtained, renewed, and revoked using the DAS Office of 
Information Technology (OIT) Digital Certificate Provisioning service. 
2. TLS certificates may also be obtained from an approved vendor. 
3. Website sessions shall be encrypted using current, supported TLS protocols.  
 
M. Website Accessibility : Agency websites shall comply with the accessibility requirements 
outlined in Ohio Administrative Policy IT-09, “Website Accessibility.” 
 
N. Domain Name Standardization : Each Agency is required to have an Ohio.gov domain in 
accordance with the requirements outlined in Ohio Administrative Policy IT -06, "Ohio.gov 
Standardization & Registration of Domain Names." 
 
O. Website Standardization Requirement Guidance : For additional website standardization 
requirement guidance, Agencies  shall refer to the InnovateOhio Platform User Experience 
Digital Toolkit web page, ux.ohio.gov. 
 
P. Identification and Authentication: Where Identification and Authentication are required for 
website functionality, Agencies shall leverage InnovateOhio Platform solutions. 
 
Q. Website and Web -Facing Application Security : Agencies shall ensure that the following

R. InnovateOhio Platform Responsibilities: The InnovateOhio Platform team shall: 
 
1. Oversee the State of Ohio’s digital presence and partner with Agencies to ensure their 
websites align with statewide requirements; 
2. Maintain digital toolkit standards and resources; 
3. Support and provide Agencies with access to a website content management tool;  
4. Communicate any substantive changes in website standards or content management to 
Agencies and content coordinators in a timely manner; and  
5. Work with the DAS OISP to institute denial of service protections.

B. Confidential Data. This classification includes Data that is required to be protected by law or 
regulation, is intended for confidential use, and may not be copied or removed from an 
agency's operational control without authorized permission.  This classification includes Data 
that, if compromised, may result in loss of life, serious injury, or other harm to an individual 
or group, or disruption to critical agency operations. 
C. Covered Applications. As defined in ORC Section 125 183, a "Covered Application” means all 
of the following: 
1. The TikTok application and service or any successor application or service developed or 
provided by ByteDance limited or an entity owned by ByteDance limited; 
2. The WeChat application and service or any successor application or service developed 
or provided by Tencent holdings limited or an entity owned by Tencent holdings 
limited; 
3. Any application or service owned by an entity located in China, including QQ 
International (QQi), Qzone, Weibo, Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book), Zhihu, Meituan, 
Toutiao, Alipay, Xiami Music, Tiantian Music, DingTalk/DingDing, Douban, Renren, 
Youku/Tudou, and Zhihu. 
D. Domain Names. A domain name is used to locate computers on the Internet and to allow users 
to reach websites. Each domain name has at least two components, the top- level domain 
(TLD) name and the secondary level domain (SLD) name. In terms of Ohio.gov, “.gov” is the 
TLD and “Ohio” is the SLD. A third level domain may be added to further distinguish a SLD. For 
the purposes of this policy, Agencies are required to have an Ohio.gov alias at the third level. 
Structurally, the domain name would conform to the following format: [Agency 
Name/Acronym].Ohio.gov. Refer to section O. , Domain Name Standardization, of this policy 
for specific domain name requirements. 
E. Identification. An act or process that presents an identifier to a system so that the system can 
recognize a system entity (e.g., user, process, or device) and distinguish that entity from all 
others.2

G. Security Purposes. As referenced in this policy, Covered Applications may be used for Security

through the identification of vulnerabilities, security weaknesses, or actionable threats.3 
H. Law Enforcement Purposes.  As referenced in this policy, Covered Application s may be used 
for Law Enforcement Purposes, which may include, but are not limited to, activities that are 
performed by or in coordination with an agency that is part of the federal or state law 
enforcement community, in response to a law enforcement emergency, or in the course of 
investigating potential violations of federal or state statutes or regulations.4  
I. Public-facing Web Page s. Public-facing Web Pages are web pages that are accessed via the 
World Wide Web by any Internet user throughout the world. Public-facing Web Pages include 
information available to the general public, but do not include intranets, extranets, e-mail, or 
applications. 
J. Social Media . Refers to an online environment or application  that facilitate s user 
participation, networking, and collaboration through the submission of user generated 
content. In general, this includes tools such as: blogs, wikis, microblogging sites, social 
networks, video sharing sites, and bookmarking sites. 
K. State-controlled Websites. Websites developed and maintained by Ohio Agencies , or under 
contracts administered by Ohio Agencies. 
L. State-Owned. Purchased with state funds or otherwise acquired by the state; property of the 
State of Ohio. 
M. Transport Layer Security (TLS). An Authentication and encryption protocol widely 
implemented in browsers and Web servers. HTTP traffic transmitted using TLS is known as
