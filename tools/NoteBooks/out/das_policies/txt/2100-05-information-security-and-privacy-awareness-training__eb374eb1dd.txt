I. Purpose 
Information security and privacy awareness training is necessary so that Organizational U sers of 
Ohio Department of Administrative Services ( DAS)-Managed Information S ystems understand 
their responsibilities to protect state systems and the information they contain. 
 
The first occurrence of the word in the policy body outside of a title or heading is in bold, italic type, 
and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV . In addition, references to National Institute of 
Standards in Technology (NIST) Special Publication (SP) 800 -53 family identifiers and control 
numbers are provided in parentheticals next to requirement headers, where applicable. The 
requirements of the NI ST SP 800-53 Awareness and Training (AT) control family are addressed in 
this policy. 
 
II. Scope 
The scope of this policy includes DAS -Managed Information System s, Service Owners, managers 
of DAS business units, and those who use or administer such systems. This policy is not intended 
for the general population that accesses State of Ohio government services or applications. 
 
III. Policy 
DAS established an information security and privacy awareness training program to assure that all 
Organizational Users, who use or operate DAS-Managed Information System s understand and 
adhere to all IT security policies. 
  
A. Basic Information Security and Privacy Awareness Training (AT- 2): DAS programs, with 
support from the DAS Office of Information Security and Privacy (OISP), shall provide ongoing 
Basic Information S ecurity and P rivacy Awareness Training for all Organizational User s.

Additional training may also be prescribed based on the data an individual has access to, 
either explicitly or incidentally, as a result of their job functions. Examples include: 
 
1. Confidential Personal Information (CPI) (Section 1347.15 of the Ohio Revised Code). 
2. Criminal Justice Information (CJI). 
3. Federal Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) Information. 
4. Federal Tax Information (FTI). 
5. Payment Card Industry (PCI) Information. 
6. Personal Health Information (PHI) or Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act 
(HIPAA) Information. 
7. Social Security Administration (SSA) Information. 
 
B. Role-Based Security and Privacy Training (AT- 3): In accordance with Ohio Administrative 
Policy IT-15, “Information Security and Privacy Awareness Training,” DAS identified the 
following job functions that require additional Role-Based Security and Privacy Training: 
 
1. Business managers and senior executives 
2. Application developers, system administrators, and database administrators 
3. Desktop support 
4. Customer Service Center representatives (help desk personnel) 
 
DAS OISP shall determine the specific nature and frequency of additional information security 
and privacy awareness training. 
 
C. Information Security and Privacy Awareness Training Records (AT- 4): General 
information security and privacy awareness training records shall be maintained in 
accordance with Ohio Administrative Policy IT -15, “Information Security and Privacy 
Awareness Training.” State records retention schedules shall be followed for all training 
records. 
 
IV. Definitions 
  
A. Basic Information Security and Privacy Awareness Training . The purpose of this type of 
training is for the participant to gain a basic understanding of the need for information 
security and privacy and the actions that he/she can take to maintain security and privacy and 
respond to suspected incidents. This training also increases awareness regarding the 
importance of operations security.

The Ohio Department of Administrative Services is the engine of state government, providing innovative solutions and 
supporting the efficient operation of state agencies, boards, and commissions. 
B. Confidential Personal Information (CPI). Personal information that falls within the scope of 
Section 1347.15 of the Ohio Revised Code and that an agency is prohibited from releasing 
under Ohio’s public records law. 
C. DAS-Managed Information System . Information systems that reside in facilities or 
infrastructure managed by DAS OIT personnel. Primary responsibility for managing these 
systems may be assigned to DAS OIT personnel or other outside entities. 
D. Organizational User. An Organizational User  is anyone who is an employee of the state or is 
otherwise authorized to work on behalf of the state, and who has a need to access State of 
Ohio IT systems and has secured agency authorization for such access. The organizational 
identifier will help to dis tinguish the individual during the authentication process. Examples 
of resources that may fall into this category include full -time employees, part-time 
employees, seasonal employees, temporary contract workers, independent contractors or 
consultants, volunteers or unpaid interns. The Organizational User  identifier category may 
also include university and federal government resources. 
E. Role-Based Security and Privacy Training. Role-based training strives to produce relevant and 
needed security and privacy knowledge and skills within the workforce. Role -Based Security 
and Privacy Training  supports competency development and helps personnel understand 
and learn how to better perform their specific security or privacy role, which ultimately 
achieves more secure and protected information and systems. 
F. Service Owner . A Service Owner is responsible for the delivery (design, performance, 
integration), continual improvement and management of assigned IT services.
