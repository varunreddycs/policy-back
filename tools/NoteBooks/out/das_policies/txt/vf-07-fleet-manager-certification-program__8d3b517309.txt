I. Purpose 
This policy establishes a fleet manager certification program (Program). The Program may be 
instituted by a third -party designated by the Ohio Department of Administrative Services Office 
of Fleet Management (OFM) and may be supplemented by information and training pr ovided by 
OFM. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked 
to the definition in Section IV. 
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to any  State Agency that has full or partial delegation of authority from OFM 
pursuant to DAS Statewide Policy VF-06/Delegation of Authority of Fleet Management.  
 
III. Policy 
 
A. Full Certification : OFM will certify a State Agency’s fleet manager as a certified fleet 
manager after the fleet manager completes the NAFA CAFM program and provided the fleet 
manager meets all the following: 
 
1. is an employee of a State Agency and is designated as the State Agency’s fleet manager 
responsible for overseeing the operation of that State Agency’s fleet 
2. receives a passing score in each of the eight disciplines of the NAFA CAFM program and 
submits their certificate of completion to OFM for verification 
 
B. Provisional Certification: DAS may provisionally certify an individual as a fleet manager 
for a period of not more than three years if the individual meets all the following:

C. Certification Program Curriculum: OFM designates the NAFA CAFM certification program 
as the method for the certification of State Agency  fleet managers. The NAFA CAFM 
certification course contains eight core competency disciplines . Individuals must pass all 
eight disciplines within a three -year period  to complete their certification. The eight 
certification disciplines are: 
 
1. Asset Management  
2. Business Management 
3. Financial Management 
4. Information Management 
5. Maintenance Management 
6. Professional Development 
7. Risk Management 
8. Vehicle Fuel Management 
 
D. General Provisions   
 
1. Each State Agency will ensure that its designated fleet manager completes the NAFA 
CAFM program no later than three years from the date of hire. 
2. Each State Agency will ensure that its designated fleet manager maintains the NAFA CAFM 
certification.  
3. To remain a certified fleet manager, a State Agency’s fleet manager must comply with 
any continuing education requirements prescribed by NAFA or OFM. 
4. The State Agency’s fleet manager will perform the duties of a fleet manager in a 
professional manner, as determined by OFM, including providing timely information to 
OFM.  
5. The State Agency’s fleet manager must adhere to all provisions concerning fleet 
management as required by the Ohio Revised Code, the Ohio Administrative Code, and 
DAS policy. 
6. Failure of a State Agency’s fleet manager to obtain initial NAFA CAFM certification or 
maintain certification may result in OFM revoking all or part of the State Agency’s 
delegated authority as set forth in Policy VF-06.
