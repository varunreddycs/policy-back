I. Purpose 
The purpose of this policy is to provide information security assessment and Ohio Department of 
Administrative Services (DAS) Office of Information Security & Privacy (OISP) security approval 
requirements for DAS-Managed Information Systems and Services. 
 
The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the 
definition in Section IV . In addition, references to National Institute of Standards in Technology 
(NIST) Special Publication (SP) 800 -53 family identifiers and control numbers are provided in 
parentheticals next to requirement headers, where applicable. The requirements of the NI ST SP 
800-53 Security Assessment and Authorization (CA) control family are addressed in this policy. 
 
II. Scope 
The scope of this policy includes all DAS-Managed Information Systems and Services. 
 
III. Policy 
A DAS OISP security approval is required for the operation of DAS -Managed Information Systems 
and Services. The security approval ensures that security controls are assessed prior to placing the 
system(s) or service(s) into operation. The systems and services are also required to be regularly 
re-approved to ensure that the appropriate security safeguards are in place. 
  
A. Information Security Assessments (CA -2): An information security assessment shall be 
completed and must include the following: 
 
1. Information Security Assessment Methodology: An information security assessment 
methodology shall be developed that describes the scope of the assessment including:

a) Data Classification:  A process that identifies what information needs to be protected

b) Privacy Assessment: A privacy assessment examines a business process or system of

c) Risk Assessment: The risk assessment practice is designed to identify potential 
threats, associated impact, and the likelihood of the threat occurring, in accordance 
with DAS Policy 2100-08, “Risk Assessment.”   
d) Security Plan: A security plan shall be developed that aligns with the security 
controls in NIST SP 800-53 (Revision 4). 
e) Penetration Testing (CA-8): Penetration Testing of Internet-facing systems shall, as 
a minimum, be conducted every two years, and annually for B usiness Critical IT 
System Assets or Services to identify any potential weaknesses in security controls. 
Penetration Testing shall be conducted on a more frequent basis when indicated by 
the results of the information system or service risk assessment. 
2. Assessment Frequency: Security controls in DAS-Managed Information Systems and 
Services shall be assessed to determine the extent to which the controls are implemented 
correctly, operating as intended, and producing the desired outcome with respect to 
meeting established security requirements. A t a minimum, the assessment frequency 
shall be: 
a) Prior to and after implementing proposed Significant Changes; 
b) After a security incident; or  
c) Every two years. 
The security plan shall be updated to include new or modified security controls.   
3. Assessment Maintenance: The results of the security controls assessment shall be 
included in the information system or service security approval package and maintained 
by DAS OISP. 
4. Assessment Independence: When DAS -managed systems and services are assessed as 
high criticality and/or high confidentiality, independent assessors and assessment teams 
shall be used to conduct security control assessments.

C. DAS OISP Security Approval : Each DAS -managed information system or service shall be 
assigned an authorizing official, who is responsible for budgetary oversight and the 
mission/business functions supported by the system or service. The authorizing official shall 
not authorize the system or service to operate until it has been granted a security approval by 
the State CISO. The information system or service security approval shall be updated every 
two years, or after a major system upgrade or service modification. 
 
The DAS OISP security approval process consists of four phases: 
 
1. Information Security Assessment Documentation: The system or service owner shall 
provide the security documentation required in section A.1 . Also, a schedule shall be 
developed by DAS OISP for the IT security review, provisional security approval, and DAS 
OISP security approval. 
2. IT Security Review: DAS OISP shall conduct a review of the documentation submitted in 
accordance with section A.1. of this policy. In addition, the system or service owner shall 
work with DAS OISP and the State of Ohio Office of Procurement Services, when 
appropriate, to ensure that the vendor completes a questionnaire provided by DAS OISP. 
DAS OISP shall also include the questionnaire response in the IT security review. 
3. Provisional Security Approval: Contingent upon a favorable DAS OISP review of the 
security documentation, a provisional security approval will be provided by DAS OISP. 
This provisional security approval will grant the system or service owner the approval t o 
move forward with development and implementation.   
If a contract is involved, the provisional security approval must occur prior to contract 
award. Any state contract which involves a vendor gaining access to state data or systems 
shall not be entered into without provisional security approval. 
4. DAS OISP Security Approval: Based upon a favorable review of the updated security plan, 
risk assessment documentation (refer to sections A.1. and A.1.c.), and remediation plan 
(refer to section B.), a DAS OISP security approval shall be granted by the State CISO to 
the authorizing official. 
 
D. Continuous Monitoring (CA- 7): DAS OISP shall develop and implement a continuous 
monitoring strategy for security controls. This strategy shall include:  
 
1. Metrics to monitor DAS-Managed Information Systems and Services.

information system risk assessment. 
 
F. Procedures: DAS OISP will provide detailed procedures to DAS entities as part of the security 
assessment and authorization process. 
 
G. Exception Process: To request an exception to one or more of the requirements outlined in 
this policy, please complete an IT Security Exception Request form.  
 
1. The form is located within the IT Enterprise Services Portal  under the "Services & 
Products" category.  
2. If you have any questions, please contact DAS OISP (refer to Section VII. Inquiries for 
contact information). 
 
IV. Definitions 
 
A. Business Critical IT System Assets or Services: Assets, systems, or services that are identified 
as having the greatest potential to impact the achievement of the organizational mission. 
B. DAS-managed Information Systems and Services: A discrete set of information resources 
organized for the collection, processing, maintenance, use, sharing, dissemination, or 
disposition of information.
1 This includes internal DAS information systems as well as

agencies. For the purposes of this policy, primary responsibility for managing these systems 
or services may be assigned to DAS OIT personnel or other outside entities. 
C. DAS-Managed System Asset : Information, hardware, software  and services required to 
support state business, and identified during the risk assessment process as assets that need 
to be protected. Primary responsibility for managing these system assets may be assigned to 
DAS OIT personnel or other outside entities.

E. Personally Identifiable Information (PII) . “Personally identifiable information” is information

interfaces).
