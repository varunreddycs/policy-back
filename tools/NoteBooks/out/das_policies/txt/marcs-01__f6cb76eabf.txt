I. Purpose 
The purpose of this policy is to formalize the process and requirements for entities to co -locate 
equipment on Multi-Agency Radio Communication System (MARCS) towers. The first occurrence 
of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section 
IV. 
 
II. Scope 
This policy applies to all entities, both public and private, seeking use of MARCS tower sites, 
including the tower, shelter,  and/or grounds owned, leased, or otherwise legally controlled by 
MARCS. 
 
III. Policy 
The overarching priority of MARCS is to preserve future necessary expansion of MARCS equipment 
on the tower structure. Any tower analysis will first consider the designed -in future MARCS 
loading and preserve it. First priority for tower co-location will be given to state agencies utilizing 
MARCS as their primary wireless radio system, and then to public safety , police, fire, and 
emergency medical service (EMS) providers. Second priority for tower co-location will be given to 
commercial wireless carriers, such as cellular or broadband, and private enterprises with wireless 
radio needs. Commercial wireless carriers and private enterprises shall not co -locate in MARCS 
shelters. No entity shall co-locate any equipment on MARCS property without the written consent 
of MARCS and without first entering into the MARCS License Agreement. 
 
A. Application Process: Entities wishing to co-locate on a MARCS tower must complete and 
submit the Tower Site Co-Location Application, Attachment A to this policy . Upon receipt 
and review of the Application, MARCS will provide an initial approval or denial of the

C. Tower Analysis and Intermodulation Study:  After receiving initial approval, entities will 
have both a Structural Tower Analysis and Intermodulation Study conducted.  
 
1. Entities shall engage an engineer to conduct a Structural Tower Analysis utilizing a 
Class III Rating. The Structural Tower A nalysis shall produce an engineer -stamped 
letter indicating the tower’s ability to hold current loads, MARCS-design future loads, 
and the additional requested loads, unless otherwise specified. This Structural Tower 
Analysis will be conducted at the entity’s expense.  
2. MARCS may conduct an Intermodulation Study at no cost to the entity , if requested. 
Otherwise, the entity must conduct the Intermodulation Study at its own expense. 
3. Entities shall provide the Structural Tower Analysis and Intermodulation Study to 
MARCS for approval and acceptance. After an entity’s application has been approved, 
copies of all studies, drawings, plan modifications, as built, and analysis shall be 
provided to MARCS. 
 
D. Technical and Site Requirements: A co-locating entity shall meet all technical and site co-
location requirements: 
 
1. Shelter Equipment 
a) Equipment shall be installed in 7’x19” equipment racks unless otherwise 
specified. 
b) Equipment shall be grounded to the interior halo ring per Motorola R56 
standard. 
c) Equipment shall be on a dedicated air conditioning (AC) breaker using the non-
uninterrupted power supply (UPS) breaker panel. 
d) AC power connections shall be installed using the ceiling electrical raceway. 
e) No equipment shall use the existing shelter wall outlets or connect to the 
MARCS UPS breaker panel. 
f) Equipment shall be installed in a manner consistent with the installation of the 
MARCS equipment. 
g) New equipment British thermal unit (BTU) loading shall be provided to MARCS 
to determine if the existing heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC)  
size is satisfactory. 
h) New equipment power loading shall be provided to MARCS to determine if the 
existing generator size is satisfactory.

2. Antenna and Transmission Lines 
a) Antennas shall be mounted using a Pirod side arm or its approved equivalent. 
b) Installation shall be completed as directed by manufacturer’s specifications. 
c) Transmission lines shall be grounded at the transmission line port following 
manufacturer’s specifications. 
d) Additional grounding shall be completed at the following locations: 
(1)  On the tower’s bottom ground buss bar 
(2) Every 200’ from the bottom ground buss bar 
(3) At the antenna base 
e) A Polyphaser coaxial protector or its approved equivalent shall be installed on 
each transmission line according to manufacturer’s specifications. 
f) Transmission lines shall be supported on the tower and transmission line 
bridge consistent with the method currently in place by MARCS. 
g) Only one transmission line per port shall be allowed unless otherwise specified. 
If needed, additional ports shall be added at co-locator’s expense. 
3. Site Entry 
a) A personnel list detailing potential site visitors shall be provided by the entity 
co-locating. This list shall include full name, date of birth , and social security 
number for each potential site visitor. Each potential visitor submitted on the 
list shall be subject to an electronic record check, and/or a ten-print fingerprint 
check, conducted by the Ohio State Highway Patrol. This list shall be u pdated 
annually or as changes occur. 
b) Sites are alarmed. All personnel shall call the MARCS Network Operations 
Center (NOC)  at 1 -866-646-2727 before entering the gate and immediately 
before departure of the compound. 
c) Anyone entering the site shall provide their name, company, contact 
information, and reason for entry. 
4. Shelter Entry 
a) Shelters are alarmed. All personnel shall call the MARCS NOC at 1-866-646-2727 
before entering the gate and immediately before departure of the compound. 
b) Anyone entering the shelter shall provide their name, company, contact 
information, and reason for entry. 
c) Shelter entry keys are available by contacting the MARCS office at 614-995-0060 
or emailing MARCS.admin@das.ohio.gov. Keys are issued after background 
checks and keys shall be reprogrammed yearly. 
5. Administrative Requirements 
a) Copies of all documentation , including a site plan for the co -location 
incorporating both the proposed equipment   and the existing MARCS facilities, 
tower/site elevations, antenna details, shelter plans (including  foundations 
and/or caissons), utilities and a grounding plan, shall be provided to MARCS by 
the entity. 
b) Any work which is to be completed on a MARCS tower site must be approved by 
the MARCS Program Director or designee.   To obtain approval, contact the 
MARCS NOC with the work to be completed, the name of the company or 
individual to complete the work, and date/time of access at least 48 hours prior

E. Fees for Co-Location 
 
1. State agencies and tier partners utilizing MARCS as their primary wireless radio system 
shall pay no fee for utilization of MARCS towers or shelters.  
2. Non-State public safety agencies shall be charged a fee of $150 per month per antenna , 
if utilizing the on -site MARCS shelter. Use of the MARCS shelter shall include MARCS 
provided commercial and back-up power.  
3. Non-State public safety agencies shall be charged a fee of $125 per month per antenna, 
if utilizing their own or other non-MARCS on-site shelter.  
4. Commercial carriers and/or other non -public safety/public service entities shall be 
charged a negotiated rate. MARCS will provide an estimate of the co-location license fee 
at the time an entity submits its application. The negotiated rate will take into 
consideration the following factors: 
a) Height of the tower; 
b) Location of the entity on the tower; 
c) Amount of load of the entity’s equipment;

IV. Definitions 
 
A. Class III Rating. Under the American National Standards Institute and Telecommunications 
Industry Association, Class III is the highest reliability rating and is assigned to structures used 
primarily for essential communications  and where a failure represents a substantial hazard 
to human life and/or damage to property.  
B. Intermodulation Study. An intermodulation study  calculates all possible frequencies that 
could potentially interfere with receivers at a communications site. 
C. Structural Tower Analysis. A structural tower analysis uses engineers to determine how a 
tower will respond to various loads and stresses to help identify the stability of the structure.
