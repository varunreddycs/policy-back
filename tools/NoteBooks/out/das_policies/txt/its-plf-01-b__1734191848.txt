                    ITS-PLF-01-B Specialty Use Class Desktop


State of Ohio IT Standard - Attachment B

Effective Date:
 10/28/2025

Effective Period:
 12 months

This standard configuration is subject to change within 12 months of its effective date. The most current version of this standard configuration (available on the DAS Technology & Strategy website) should be obtained when making IT planning or acquisition decisions.

Profile for Specialty Use Class Desktop

Category Description

This category is intended to fill the requirements for specific job purposes. This category offers significant capacity and the expandability required to meet demanding, high-end computing requirements.
The acquisition of this configuration is limited to certain use cases and requires that a business justification be attached to each purchase request.

Category Use Cases

Developers who design and maintain mission critical applications.

System personnel who operate and support mission critical services or large-scale infrastructures.

Users who work with software applications including, but not limited to, geographic information systems (GIS), spatial analysis, scientific analysis, computer-aided design, complex graphic design, video editing and creation, large-scale data modeling, statistical analysis, and data mining.

Planned Service Life

Desktops in this category are expected to have a planned service life of five years. Agencies acquiring this category should estimate the total cost of ownership over a five-year period. Systems are expected to be acquired with a four-year onsite, next-business-day warranty from the manufacturer.

Manageability

Desktops that conform to this category can be centrally managed and locked down to achieve the lowest total cost of ownership. These desktops are capable of having a factory-installed software image; however, there may be one or more custom applications that are installed after the system is received. They can be supported remotely and diagnosed by agencies with the appropriate management tools. Intel® vPro features with Intel® Active Management Technology are recommended.
