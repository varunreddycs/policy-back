                  Emergency and Disaster Relief Service Leave (HR-20)


August 29, 2023

 |

Agency

State of Ohio Administrative Policy

No: HR-20 -
 State Human Resources Division

Effective:
 September 30, 2025

Issued By:
Kathleen C. Madden, Director

Purpose

To provide State of Ohio employees with paid leave or other options when the employee must be absent from work to provide volunteer emergency service and/or disaster service relief and recovery efforts. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked to the definition in Section IV.

Scope

This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).

Policy

Pursuant to Ohio Revised Code (ORC) section 124.1310 (emergency service leave) and section 124.132 (disaster service leave), each calendar year the State of Ohio may provide eligible state employees with paid leave to provide
Emergency Medical Services
, volunteer
Fire-Fighting Services
, disaster relief services, or team rubicon services.

Emergency Service Leave

Eligibility: All full-time and part-time permanent employees paid by warrant of the Director of Budget and Management are eligible for emergency service leave pursuant to ORC section 124.1310 if the employee meets the certification requirements for an EMT-basic, EMT-I (Intermediate), first responder, paramedic, or volunteer firefighter. Employees shall notify their Agency of any changes to the employee’s status as a volunteer EMT-basic, EMT-I, first responder, paramedic, or firefighter.

Leave and Benefit Amount: An eligible state employee shall receive 120 hours of paid leave each calendar year when the employee is absent from work providing either Emergency Medical Services or Fire-Fighting Services or when the employee is absent from work attending a training or continuing education program that relates to providing those services.

Employees using leave under this policy shall be compensated at the regular rate of pay.

Employees using leave under this policy remain in active pay status and continue accruing vacation, personal, and sick leave at the regular rate of pay.

Verification Documentation:

Each time an employee uses emergency service leave, the employee must provide written documentation from the volunteer fire department’s chief, medical director, or chief administrator of the cooperating medical service organization’s physician advisory board, as applicable. The documentation must verify the dates and amount of time the employee spent providing either Emergency Medical Services or Fire-Fighting Services or attending a training or continuing education program related to those services and describe the activity(-ies), including the name of the training or continuing education program, if applicable.

Employees who volunteer as an EMT-basic, EMT-I, first responder, paramedic, or volunteer firefighter must submit a valid certificate to their Agency in accordance with ORC section 4113.41(B)(1).

Disaster Service Leave

Eligibility: All full-time and part-time permanent employees paid by warrant of the Director of Budget and Management who are certified disaster service volunteers of the American red cross or verified team rubicon volunteers when they are requested by the American red cross or team rubicon to provide disaster relief services.

Leave and Benefit Amount: Upon the approval of that employee's appointing authority, an eligible state employee may be granted leave from work with pay not to exceed 30 workdays each calendar year for disaster relief services or team rubicon services pursuant to ORC section 124.132.

Employees using leave under this policy shall be compensated at the regular rate of pay.

Employees using leave under this policy remain in active pay status and continue accruing vacation, personal, and sick leave at the regular rate of pay.

Verification Documentation: Each time an employee requests to use disaster service leave, the employee must provide written documentation from either the American red cross or team rubicon. The documentation must verify the dates and amount of time the employee spent providing the services and describe the services requested and provided by the employee.

Requests for Leave

Employees requesting leave under this policy must request the leave as soon as practicable. Each Agency may designate the procedure for requesting this type of leave. If the Agency does not designate a procedure, the leave may be requested using the Agency’s standard procedure for requesting leave. Employees must indicate the request is for emergency service leave and/or disaster service leave.

Employees who previously scheduled an absence using other forms of leave shall not be permitted to substitute emergency service leave or disaster service leave for the type of leave already scheduled. For example, if an employee performs volunteer fire-fighting duties on a previously scheduled vacation day, the employee will be charged for vacation.

Illness or Injury Due to Leave

Employees who experience medical complications as a result of using emergency service leave or disaster service leave may still apply for other state benefits (e.g., disability) if they meet all eligibility criteria.

Other Leave Options

The State of Ohio recognizes that in the event of a national disaster, employees may have questions about taking leave to personally assist in the relief efforts. Nothing in this policy prevents an employee from requesting to use their own vacation, compensatory, or personal leave balances, subject to Agency approval in accordance with the ORC, the OAC, Agency policy, and any applicable collective bargaining agreement.

Definitions

Emergency Medical Service:
 Any of the services described in ORC sections 4765.35, 4765.37, 4765.38, and 4765.39 that are performed by first responders, emergency medical technicians-basic, emergency medical technicians-intermediate (EMT-I), and paramedics, including such services performed before or during any transport of a patient, including transports between hospitals and transports to and from helicopters. See ORC section 4765.01(G).

Fire-Fighting Service:
 Any firefighting performed by an individual in their capacity as a volunteer firefighter as defined in ORC section 146.01.

Authority

ORC 124.132, 124.1310, 4113.41

The Ohio Department of Administrative Services is the engine of state government, providing innovative solutions and 
supporting the efficient operation of state agencies, boards, and commissions. 
 
 
I. Purpose 
To add a temporary addendum to State of Ohio Policy HR-20/Emergency Service Leave to do the 
following: encourage Agencies to remind employees of Policy HR -20 and advise them that 
employees covered by HR -20 may  use Emergency Service Leave  to provide certain services to 
public entities in the state that need assistance before, during, and after the Solar Eclipse ; 
encourage Agencies to provide State of Ohio employees who are Peace Officers with up to 40 hours 
of Emergency Service Leave to provide Volunteer Services to public entities in the state that need 
assistance before, during, and after the  Solar Eclipse; and to suspend the application of Section 
III.C.3. of HR-20 to those employees using Emergency Service Leave pursuant to HR-20 to provide 
services to public entities in the state that need assistance before, during, and after the Solar 
Eclipse. The first occurrence of a defined term in the policy is in bold, italic type, and is hyperlinked 
to the definition in Section IV.  
 
II. Scope 
This addendum applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the 
Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).  
 
III. Policy 
The State of Ohio is providing Emergency Service Leave to the appointing authority of Agencies 
that determine its employees who are Peace Officers will be eligible for such leave.  
 
A. Eligibility: All full -time and part -time employees paid by warrant of the Director of Budget 
and Management are eligible for Emergency Service Leave if the employee is a Peace Officer.  
 
B. Leave Amount and Use

1. An eligible state employee will receive 40 hours of paid leave. 
2. Eligible state employees may use the 40 hours of paid leave between the dates of April 5, 
2024, and April 9, 2024, only. 
3. The leave must be used to provide Volunteer Services to public entities in the state that 
need assistance relating to the Solar Eclipse. 
 
C. Request to Use Leave 
 
1. Employees using leave under this addendum are required to follow the appointing 
authority’s policies and procedures for employees who are unable to report to work 
because they are providing Volunteer Services to a public entity in the state relating to 
the Solar Eclipse.  
2. Employees requesting paid Emergency Service Leave must use the Agency’s standard 
procedure for requesting leave as soon as practicable. Employees must indicate the 
request is for Emergency Service Leave and provide verified documentation as described 
below. 
 
D. Verified Documentation: When an employee uses Emergency Service Leave, the employee 
must provide a written statement from the public entity’s appointing authority verifying that 
the employee provided Volunteer Services to the public entity relating to the Solar Eclipse. 
 
E. Agency Adoption of Policy Addendum: Any Agency that determines to adopt this addendum 
can do so by having the Appointing Authority send an email to 
das.hrd.hr.operations@das.ohio.gov and providing a copy of this addendum to its employees. 
 
IV. Definitions 
 
A. Emergency Service Leave . Forty hours of paid leave to eligible state employees when 
providing Volunteer Services to public entities within the state relating to the Solar Eclipse.  
B. Peace Officer. As defined in Ohio Revised Code (ORC) section 2935.01(B).  
C. Solar Eclipse. The solar eclipse that will occur within the state of Ohio on April 8, 2024. 
D. Volunteer Services. Services provided without receiving any renumeration.
